# Solid-only C19 material feedback pilot

This separate native component evolves the existing two-foil A_z magnetoquasistatic current-constrained solver with online sealed material-v2 conductivity and native enthalpy/thermal conductivity. Water, emission, plasma, metal vapor, mechanics and radiation are off. It does not implement Hall total-EM or a full plasma breakdown run.

The copied quick-run geometry is 5 mm foil width,0.2 mm thickness,5 mm gap,25 mm invariant length and a true-vacuum exterior bounded atplus/minus 40 mm. The current is the bounded, unshifted C19 plot-digitized trace, starting at its first supported knot. Initial state is a declared 300 K uniform-temperature DC state at that current; prior heating/field history is unknown. Conductor gradients are solved from the two current constraints; predicted loop voltage is an output. No remembered voltage waveform is also imposed.

Each accepted interval reassembles sigma(T_accepted) in the EM mass operator, conductor loads and current Schur complement. The solved E_z=g-dA_z/dt and that same conductivity/quadrature supply J=sigma*E_z and Joule=sigma*E_z^2. Two native thermal trials use the exact same physical Joule input, nonlinear h(T), frozen-start k(T), phase and latent heat. Thermal discretization is the qualified adapter's mass-lumped nodal enthalpy with conservative dual-cell harmonic conduction. Both preflight before committing. The next interval uses returned T-dependent sigma. This is first-order accepted-start property coupling, not a converged nonlinear midpoint iteration.

Material selection is explicitly q2 source_conditioned_liquid_3000 at reference density 8000 kg/m3, fixed reference geometry/mass and Cp-as-Cv approximation. It is a source-conditioned nominal model, not measured foil calibration. Bounds remain 298.2..3000K, solidus 1672K and liquidus 1727K. The independent 773.15K mechanics data limit is reported; no elastic mechanics is applied beyond it or anywhere in this pilot. An insulated fixed-shape liquid electrode is a conditional thermal component, not a prediction of actual liquid morphology or material loss.

The solver retains the reference CG residual tolerances and current guard. It verifies current reconstruction, matching EM/thermal Joule quadrature, terminal work=magnetic change+Joule+backward-Euler magnetic numerical damping, and thermal/combined energy balances. Numerical magnetic damping stays separate from wall heat. Every accepted interval writes a common two-thermal-state/field checkpoint, published through CURRENT only after all files are written. Rebuilding the trajectory from committed row records excludes uncommitted output. Native checkpoint replay is checked byte for byte against an uninterrupted eight-step prefix. Full filesystem crash durability and a full multi-component checkpoint are not claimed.

On an unsupported trial, accepted heat and fields remain at the previous common checkpoint. Candidate fields and a bounded-domain residual certificate are retained separately. The certificate solves the same conservative caloric/conductive equations constrained to the material data bounds, then reports unresolved hot/cold joules on active bounds. It never commits those diagnostic bound temperatures or extrapolates the model. The certificate identity is delta_thermal=Joule-unsupported_hot+unsupported_cold, with explicit interior/identity arithmetic residuals. This quantifies the rejected-step excess, not heat removed from a continuing run.

Build with CMake/MFEM4.10/MPI in the frozen runtime, one job. The executable arguments are OUTPUT NX NY OUTSIDE GAP MAX_DT_S MAX_STEPS. Maximum interval 250ps, at most 8 steps or 15 seconds per invocation. run_pilots.py releases the CPU 7/nice 15 testing-core7 lease between chunks. The baseline quick mesh 128x16/80outside/48gap is coarsened to 32x4/20outside/12gap and 64x8/40outside/24gap, with unchanged geometry/domain. 64x8 also halves the maximum interval to 125 ps. Coarse sharp-corner peaks/guard times are resolution dependent; these two meshes do not establish spatial convergence. analyze_pilots.py compares only actual common accepted times and retains the raw CSV values.

Parent Hall/total-field coordination and limitations are in outputs/ss304-material-feedback-pilot-20261029/component-contract.json. The parent owns integration and production. This package does not edit shared source, another candidate or any GPU run.
