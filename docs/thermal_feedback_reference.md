# Local thermal feedback from desorption

This reference couples the temperature of one stationary patch to release from
one finite parent reservoir. Every coefficient is explicitly manufactured.
The patch has a supplied constant areal heat capacity and receives zero external
power. The reference isolates thermal feedback before a material or host model
is available; it includes no conduction, phase change, radiation, mechanical
work, moving surface or gap evolution.

## Equations and energy reference

For areal inventory `N`, temperature `T`, fixed reference coverage `Nref` and
constant attempt frequency `nu`, the declared kinetic law is

```text
Ea(N) = Ed * (1 - alpha*N/Nref)
Gamma = nu*N*exp[-Ea(N)/(k_B*T)]
dN/dt = -Gamma
C_A*dT/dt = -Gamma*(D + I + 2*k_B*T)
```

The remaining-population prefactor is an explicit first-order hypothesis;
it does not replace or reinterpret the dissertation's printed initial-population
prefactor.

`C_A` is supplied in J m⁻² K⁻¹. It is not inferred from foil thickness,
mass-specific heat capacity or a phase fit. `Nref` is a required independent
kinetic convention; changing the initial amount does not redefine it.
The nonnegative activation barrier must hold throughout the declared coverage
domain.

`D` is a separately supplied constant removal cost per parent, and `I` is its
fixed outgoing internal energy in the declared common energy reference.
The coverage-dependent activation barrier determines the rate; it does not
determine either energy debit. No molecular level, branching, dissociation or
collision data are generated by this reference.

The outgoing translational term `2*k_B*T` is the continuum expectation for a
thermal flux through a stationary surface. It is distinct from a bulk isotropic
energy average and from the realized energy of a finite packet sample. A
thermal velocity distribution would additionally require the supplied species
mass and the stated accommodation assumption. Reaction-fragment velocities and
temperature-dependent internal-state populations require separate owner inputs.

With bound-reference energy `Uads = -D*N`, the conserved zero-power total is

```text
C_A*(T-T0) - D*N + E_gas = -D*N0
E_gas = E_translation + E_internal
N + N_emitted = N0
```

Release increases the bound-reference energy by `D*N_emitted` while the wall
thermal energy falls. Internal and translational gas energy remain distinct
channels. The implementation must expose their signed residuals separately:
a small total residual alone cannot establish correct allocation. Patch area
converts each areal number or energy to an extensive quantity exactly once.

## Independent joint reference

Eliminating time from the two evolution equations gives

```text
b = 2*k_B/C_A
A = (D+I)/(2*k_B)
T(N) = T0 + (T0+A)*expm1[b*(N-N0)]
t(N) = integral_N^N0 dn / Gamma(n, T(n))
```

The first relation is analytic; monotone inversion of the second supplies an
independent time trajectory on a supported positive-temperature interval.
At `Ed=0`, the number history also has the exact form `N0*exp(-nu*t)`.
These relations test the joint thermal and inventory evolution rather than
only its energy bookkeeping. Quadrature/inversion estimates and temporal
refinement are numerical evidence, not rigorous interval error certificates.

The numerical reference evolves inventory and temperature independently using
fixed-step fourth-order Runge–Kutta stages, together with emitted number and
the separate energy channels. It does not substitute the analytic `T(N)`
relation into its evolution. The caller must declare supported temperature
and coverage ranges. Unsupported stages or unresolvable positive changes are
rejected; inventories, temperature and rare positive transfers are not clipped
or replaced with floors.

Passing those guards establishes supported arithmetic and stage values, rather
than a temporal-accuracy certificate. Accuracy requires separate refinement
and reference comparisons for the declared inputs.

## Interface and manufactured batch

The [module](../mhdlab/thermal_feedback_reference.py) exposes
`FeedbackReservoir`, `FeedbackProblem`, `feedback_rhs` and `evolve_feedback`.
All reservoir, thermal-domain, patch-area, energy-reference and provenance
parameters are required. An existing public `TemkinReservoir` can supply a
structural parameter copy; its evolution methods are not invoked. Histories
contain immutable samples with separate areal and extensive quantities and
seven signed number/energy residuals.

Run the explicit [manufactured configuration](../configs/thermal_feedback_manufactured.json)
into a new directory:

```sh
python tools/run_thermal_feedback_reference.py --config configs/thermal_feedback_manufactured.json --output-dir runs/thermal_feedback_reference --plot
```

The six cases exercise a zero barrier, a constant barrier, a coverage-dependent
barrier with `Nref` distinct from `N0`, translation-only cooling, weak release
and exactly empty inventory. Nine trajectories include the nonlinear case at
8, 16, 32 and 64 steps. The report retains parameters, code/configuration and
artifact hashes, SI units, channels and finite refinement changes. CSV/JSON
histories and optional PNG/PDF figures remain outside the source tree.

The command refuses existing output destinations and retains incomplete
staging evidence if a solve fails. JSON numeric literals that overflow or
lose a nonzero value to zero during conversion are rejected before evaluation.
The fixtures' times, temperatures and inventories have no registration to
the recovered dissertation figures.

## Verification evidence

An independent Decimal implementation reads the saved histories without
importing the core or calling its producer. It checks the analytic thermal
curve and individual number/energy relations across 289 samples, with 3,757
CSV/JSON scalar comparisons, 3,468 area projections and 32 selected forward
time-map checks. A final-time inverse is reused across each case's refinement
grids. The reference uses exact binary projections of supplied parameters and
exact declared SI decimal constants, with generated Decimal quadrature nodes.
Changes between 80-digit/24-node and 120-digit/48-node calculations are retained
as numerical estimates.

For the nonlinear 64-step case, endpoint errors relative to each quantity's
own change or emitted-energy budget are approximately:

| Quantity | Absolute error / declared quantity budget |
| --- | --- |
| Emitted number | 7.32 × 10⁻¹¹ |
| Temperature decrease | 5.33 × 10⁻¹⁰ |
| Outgoing translation | 9.97 × 10⁻¹⁰ |

The 8–64-step family has finite self-order estimates of approximately
4.157–4.934 across eight quantities. The largest endpoint error anywhere in
that family is about 5.80 × 10⁻⁶ of its own channel budget at eight steps.
The largest signed-ledger magnitude is about 4.42 × 10⁻¹¹ of the thermal-debit
budget, in the weak-release case. These are model-specific numerical checks;
they supply no general accuracy certificate or physical uncertainty.

The focused tests preserve their exact code versions: 34 cases passed before
a two-line input-validation correction, followed by five new negative-exact-value
regressions and one affected empty-inventory control on the corrected core.
The correction changes no evolution or energy equation. Initial and corrected
parser checks similarly retain their own source versions. The final saved
nine-trajectory batch and independent reference bind the corrected source.

In the explicitly manufactured nonlinear example, the independent reference
cools from 900 K to approximately 823.177357 K over 200 ns and releases
8.03318 × 10¹⁹ parents m⁻². An independently computed, externally maintained
900 K control releases 9.00622 × 10¹⁹ parents m⁻². Cooling reduces release by
about 10.8% of the maintained-temperature result. Maintaining temperature requires
external heat; it is a different energy hypothesis. The example quantifies
the numerical feedback mechanism and supplies no measured water-release effect.

Translation is accumulated as `integral 2*k_B*T*Gamma dt`; assigning the final
wall temperature to all earlier emissions would discard their energy history.

This local reference does not install feedback into an existing source history,
FLASH or a host transaction. It supplies no actual-water parameters, monolayer
coverage inference, experimental clock registration or validated foil closure.
