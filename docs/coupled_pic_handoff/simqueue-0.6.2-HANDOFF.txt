DECISIONS TAKEN
  - Line position of a queued job = exact submit time from the birth time
    (statx) of its log file, created empty in the submit transaction;
    fallback: mtime inside the created_at second; else end of that second.
    (created_at has 1 s resolution; the earlier "+0.999" made helpers that
    started up to 1 s after a submit go first.)
  - Deadlock test split into phases with raw users of one lock order each:
    raw users of different orders deadlock among themselves regardless of
    the queue (seen in a run: core,l1,l2 vs l2,l1).
  - Test outside users run in their own session; tearDown kills the group
    (a failed run had leaked blocked inner flock processes).
  - Claim units are named simq-job-gpuclaim-<token>.service (unit prefix
    simq-job); they show in `systemctl --user list-units 'simq-job-*'`.
  - upgrade.py backs up simqborrow.slice too and leaves it in place.
  - HANDOFF.txt is excluded from the seal (it changes after sealing) and is
    not copied into the Power-Flow repository.
  - (fourth worker) run-tests.py uses the temporary directory by its
    resolved path; --shared for cores 6-7 (queue jobs of powerflow are no
    reason to stop; the lease protocol arbitrates); --wait-any --min-cores N
    (looks at /proc/locks, holds nothing while waiting).
  - test_exclusive_mode.py runs as a pure suite (needs 4 CPUs in the mask).
  - Readiness correction in place in the installed 0.6.2 runtime (not a new
    version): installed 15 minutes earlier, not yet committed anywhere;
    resealed; worker restarted once; rollback backup to 0.6.1 unaffected.
  - release.json records the install (installed: true) and the correction,
    so the seal matches the final files.
  - Check jobs used --gpu-mem (shared) only: an exclusive check job would
    have made other sessions' GPU jobs wait for a drain.
