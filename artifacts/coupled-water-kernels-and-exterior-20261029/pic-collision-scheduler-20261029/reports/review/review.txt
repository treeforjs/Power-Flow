1. [P1] A smaller packet debit can disappear in the larger remainder.
Header lines108–113 construct a double remainder by original_weight-w.
The probe uses target weight1e20 and projectile weight1. It gets remainder1e20,
actual represented target debit0, event_weight1, and prepare admits the event.
The real-arithmetic propensity identity does not prevent this binary64 loss.
A native transaction consuming this remainder could create products without
representably debiting the target. Do not hide this with a tolerance scaled to
the gross1e20 inventory. Bind original weights and preserve an error-free
remainder/count ledger, or reject contrasts whose debit cannot be admitted by
the downstream representation. Test both weight orderings, no-op subtraction,
near-equal weights, subnormal counts and the native one-double conversion policy.

2. [P1] Pair limit does not bound channel work or candidate memory.
Lines97–113 limit only colocated packet pairs; the channel loop and out.push_back
have no independent query/row budget. One pair with pair_limit1 produces128
matching queries and128 candidate rows in the probe. A large channel catalogue
can multiply the advertised work/memory bound. Add checked query/work and
candidate-row limits, validate before invoking queries/push_back, and bound the
copy/sort in prepare before allocation. Avoid overflow in budget arithmetic.
The state eligibility check correctly precedes each cross-section query; a
state-indexed channel catalogue can also avoid scanning every channel per pair.

3. [P1] Separate the SSA subflow clock from the physical orbit/gather stage.
Clock lines42–46 and event_time_s lines166–169 use the accepted begin/end
interval, while positions are held fixed in a collision subflow. An SSA sample
inside that interval is not evidence that the native orbit reached that global
time/position. A synchronized midpoint insertion adapter must not stamp
products, field samples, current segments or electrode sources with that
sampled subflow time as though a drift happened. Add a frozen spatial/physical
stage ID/time and binding, accepted interval/revision, and a separately named
collision-subflow elapsed coordinate. Validate stage containment and binding,
advance only the subflow coordinate between collision events, and recompute
rates after admitted velocity/species changes. The physical driver must own
pre/post drift deposition and one accepted clock/rollback boundary. The README
already warns that the insertion stage is missing; the API needs to encode it.

4. [P2] Target-rest energy loses cancellation at extreme common boosts.
The boost subtraction at lines58–70 is algebraically correct, but not numerically
qualified for all finite proper velocities currently accepted. Exactly comoving
u=(1e20,.37e20,-.21e20) m/s yields E_target=5.519937523e9 eV with Moller flux0;
the exact target-rest kinetic energy is0. Still higher common boosts also return
large spurious energies. A zero-flux support query can therefore receive the
wrong energy. Use a cancellation-resistant nonnegative gamma_rel-1 invariant,
or explicitly fail closed outside an admitted kinematic range; test identical,
nearly comoving, radial and non-collinear velocities under large common boosts.
Do not clamp a negative radicand or a false positive energy into support.
The ordinary stationary-target8keV and modest common-boost tests are sound;
this finding does not assert a water-range rate error.

5. [P2] Raw owner address plus generation does not identify owner lifetime.
Proposal lines123–124 and can_publish line176 accept a token when its owner
address and generation match. In the probe, optional<Scheduler> is emplaced,
a proposal is prepared, then the owner is reset and emplaced with another seed
in the same storage. The new owner accepts the old token (generation0/address
match), permitting stale RNG publication. Use an independent per-instance
lifetime token retained by proposals; restart must create a fresh lifetime and
invalidate pending proposals while preserving accepted RNG/generation state.
Retain current double-publication, foreign-owner and stale-generation rejection.
Physical state/mesh/mechanism validation remains a separate parent obligation;
can_publish currently validates neither a changed physical revision nor stage.

What is correct and what is established
For weights Wp,Wt and event weight w=min(Wp,Wt), the propensity
  a=max(Wp,Wt)*sigma(E_target)*g_Moller/V
satisfies w*a=Wp*Wt*sigma*g/V in exact arithmetic. This is the correct
instantaneous represented reaction-count expectation for the proposed weighted
jump construction. The conditional branch ratio is correct for competing
exclusive channels of the same pair. It does not prove physical fluctuation
statistics or finite-packet convergence: an event contributes variance w^2*a*dt,
so the packet-weight choice controls noise. The README correctly retains that
limit and requires rates rebuilt after each admitted event/depletion.

Target-rest projectile kinetic energy and simulation-frame Moller flux are the
appropriate distinct inputs for a target-rest cross section with lab-frame
packet densities/volume. The code's formulas are mathematically consistent;
Moller flux may exceed c and is not an individual particle speed. Extrusion
volume is used once. No plotting slab is duplicated as a reacting population.

Channel state matching, exclusive-event classification, identity uniqueness,
syntactic data/kernel hashes, same-cell locality, and mandatory support query
even at zero flux are useful fail-closed controls. Query functions own thresholds,
gaps and valid incident-energy ranges; hash-shaped strings do not prove a
qualified differential product law. The manufactured constant cross sections
and placeholder hashes remain correctly labeled nonphysical H2O evidence.

The focused probe also confirms propensity overflow rejection, nonzero
propensity underflow rejection, and rejection when event time rounds onto an
interval endpoint. The stable low-energy stationary-target calculation at
u=1e-4 m/s matches its reference exactly. Open uniforms, canonical candidate
ordering, copied RNG during prepare and accepted RNG/generation checkpointing
are coherent within the frozen standard-library implementation. Preparation
alone does not publish RNG. A no-event interval still needs common acceptance
through its endpoint; the physical restart must bind all owners together.
