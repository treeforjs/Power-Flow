   The rate is now reconstructed from the bound original beforeweights, rather
   than rounded event_weight+remainder. This resolves v1's silent lost debit as
   a candidate representation, not as a complete native inventory implementation.

2. Pair/query/candidate limits: builder limits are independent and enforced.
