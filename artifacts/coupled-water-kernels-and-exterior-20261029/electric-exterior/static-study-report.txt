Scientific comparator audit

The weighted norms use positive captured particle weights. Relative L2 is
sqrt(sum(w|delta E|²)/sum(w|Ebaseline|²)); parallel and perpendicular use the
same per-particle B direction. A separate math.fsum calculation, using
delta E dot B and the perpendicular norm identity, reproduces final-pair
statistics within operation-scale roundoff. There are no zero-B samples.
Both x/y nested-axis overlap checks pass. Missing/truncated/nonfinite arrays
and any B-byte difference fail closed. Inputs are hashed before/after reading.

Gap means x 2..7 mm,y 2..7 mm; back means x 2..7 mm,y>7.2 mm. Corner vacuum
means outside both finite foil rectangles and within 250 µm of one of their
eight 2Dcorners. It is not an all-face or 3Daxial-edge mask. These masks overlap
and include ALL z positions; the separate midplane mask is abs(z)<0.5 mm.
They do not establish emission origin. A midplane density image is not the
same sampling population as these full-height gap/back norms. No density was
generated, relabeled or normalized. Field plots use exact V/m→MV/m conversion.
