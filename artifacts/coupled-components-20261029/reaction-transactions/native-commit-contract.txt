Native event/deposition/thermal commit contract - integration boundary

Implemented proposal interface
  Transactions::prepare(event) validates the complete local event and produces
  an immutable before/after snapshot without changing the owner. integration_ports
  binds that proposal to a declared AcceptedInterval: step, begin/end, spatial
  cell, deposition basis, reaction/PIC/neutral/EM/material input-prefix SHA256s.
  The event/payload/catalog IDs and all product state/source lineage travel with
  the proposal. Examples are tests/typed-integration-example.json and
  tests/typed-integration-Jz-example.json. All example prefixes are manufactured.

  ChargedPointTerm is a signed removal/insertion at one point, with q/e, mass,
  represented weight, proper velocity and state ID. The incoming primary is
  removed once; the same-ID scattered replacement and all charged products are
  inserted together. Attachment removes the free electron and inserts an anion.
  NeutralParentDebit is the SAME single reservation in PreparedReaction, not a
  second instruction to debit the neutral host or the adsorbed surface source.
  Neutral products retain the source/event/state IDs and resolved recoil.

  InternalEnergyTransfer separates electron/ion/neutral translation, state
  internal and reference changes. These sum to the numeric residual for this
  closed nonradiative event. External event work, photons and additional wall
  heat are explicitly zero in this prototype. The reference cost and resolved
  recoil must not also be applied as gas or electrode heat. PIC electron energy
  must not be evolved/heated by a duplicate Raikou F0 operator.

Required native work, not implemented or qualified here
  1. One canonical accepted-interval owner must import the ACTUAL current
     particle, state, neutral inventory and clocks. Existing GapPic only exposes
     const particles; add_particle records authored external charge/energy and
     is unsuitable for an internal reaction. Provide an internal batched
     removal/replacement/insertion interface with no authored-source increment,
     or adopt a common arena owning native particles and sidecars together.
     Do not maintain two independently evolving particle or neutral inventories.

  2. Reserve native uint64 particle IDs, sidecar state/source/event IDs, vector
     capacity, deposition storage and every partner's energy/source receipt
     before publication. Validate all q,m,weight,u/K, geometry/conductor/axial
     positions and resource limits. Newly created fast electrons/species require
     the intended gyro/plasma/displacement and particle-count guards. Reject or
     retry the complete accepted interval if any guard or reservation fails;
     do not keep a parent debit after a rejected native/EM/material stage.

  3. Under one exclusive accepted-step generation, preflight ALL prepared owner
     versions, then perform a no-fail common publication or swap one combined
     owner snapshot. can_publish()/try_publish() currently protect only this
     reaction owner. Sequentially calling unrelated mutating owners, each of
     which can fail, is not an atomic coupled commit. Keep diagnostic/event
     output after acceptance; its failure policy must not create a partial
     physical state. Stale prepared tokens are rejected without mutation.

  4. Stage all signed charges with the SAME actual spatial basis and event point
     before any field gather/solve. The local common-shape witness has zero net
     reaction-created charge for ionization, dissociation and attachment. It is
     not native Q1 deposition validation. Verify native nodal rho before/after,
     continuity, species/source ledgers, and first/second-half collision timing.

  5. Rebuild the actual accepted-interval particle current with every charged
     primary/secondary/ion/anion velocity and trajectory. The typed three-vector
     q*w*v moment difference has units C*m/s; it is NOT J[A/m2], an EM grid
     operator, or path-integrated charge transfer. In particular, zero net charge
     creation does not imply zero current change, and Jz is not zero by default.
     The Hall EM owner must supply compatible vector deposition/gather,
     stagger/time-centering, boundary/circuit ports and field feedback. Do not
     insert an ad hoc Hall force or diffusion coefficient into the transaction.

  6. Connect the heat/material owner with explicit degree-of-freedom receipts.
     Resolved ion/neutral recoil and state/reference changes above are internal
     redistribution, not deposited wall heat. Adsorbed-water release needs its
     single inventory and adsorption/translation/internal/support energy debit.
     Surface collection needs actual incident kinetic/internal/reference and
     recombination/reflection/secondary partitions. Native incident K alone is
     not deposited heat. Temperature/material changes must feed the next source
     and boundary update through the common accepted state. The present zero
     additional-wall-heat field is a limitation of closed volume events, not a
     physical assertion that the coupled foil has no heat exchange.

  7. Persist native particles, species/state ID mapping, full reaction prefix,
     neutral/source owners, EM and heat/material state as ONE accepted epoch.
     Use one versioned bundle or an atomic final manifest after durable hashed
     members. The current sealed checkpoint stores no species/state sidecar;
     do not append unversioned records to it. This standalone transaction restart
     replays reaction-only evolution from its fixture genesis; it is not a full
     moving-PIC/field/material restart and must not replay over a different
     native force history. Bind actual owner-prefix hashes, not example labels.

  8. Freeze physical channel data and selection outside this module. Required
     inputs include supported incident/relative-energy range, exclusive products,
     states, probabilities and recoil/secondary distributions with complete
     energy ownership. No rate, threshold, RGA composition or equilibrium EEDF
     fallback is supplied here. The strict-support sampling/data owner handles
     that admission. Missing support must block the affected physical model.
