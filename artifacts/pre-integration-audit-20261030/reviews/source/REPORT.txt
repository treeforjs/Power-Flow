Confirmed numerical defects

C1 (P1): source-pool debit loss. The canonical source inventory still uses two binary64 limbs. Actual Coupler::prepare emits a positive packet weight 1.0000000000000024e-30 from inventory {1e15,-0.01}, while BOTH candidate inventory limbs remain unchanged. The defect is in the source debit, before native gas credit. Gas ExactParticleCount can preserve an admitted packet exactly but cannot recover a debit already lost upstream. Its 2^-1074-unit encoding retains admitted represented weights; it is not a physical emission quantum or an automatic repair for other owners. Source debit and the actual gas packet credits require one exact represented-count transaction or an explicit bounded rejection. Do not build a second count backend.

C2 (P1): allocation does not preserve the exact debit. Actual preparation fully releases inventory 1 into ten positive packets and passes its floating assigned==released guard. Exact downstream gas arithmetic faithfully retains this excess. Validate actual parts and their total exactly; a rounded accumulator is insufficient.

C4 (P1 numerical domain): positive weight alone does not imply representable charge/energy. A valid 300 K thermal birth with weight about 1e-310 succeeds, with a positive particle-count increment but zero weighted particle charge and zero wall heat. Its physical charge is about 1.602e-329 C, below binary64's smallest positive subnormal. Retain nonzero contributions in a supported representation or reject the proposal with quantified unresolved scope. Never silently floor the source, automatically change packet weight, or declare a zero physical flux.
