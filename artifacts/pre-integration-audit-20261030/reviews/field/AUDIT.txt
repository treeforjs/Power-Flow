Hall/EM/thermal and source-v3 audit before full integration

Disposition: changes required before full integration testing. Three current
admission defects are P1: mutable candidate fields/heat are not rebound at final
preflight, initial absolute Gauss consistency is not established, and supplied
fields with nonzero divB are admitted. A separate P1 gate keeps source/boundary
population changes disabled. The midpoint-array discrepancy is P2 in observed
size but must be resolved before claiming v3's exact common-field contract.

The identity-envelope correction remains the separate published version
c2f81ab87ddc295e7bdad808d33135fa781b3942. No reviewed implementation was edited.
No native/SSA/source/field/heat common step or full coupled test ran. The only
new execution used isolated field operators and eight single native-Boris
kicks. It created no canonical arena, source population, IDs or accepted commit.

A01 [P1, current defect] Final preflight does not bind proposed field/heat data

Evidence: H/axial_joint_v2.hpp:21-26 exposes field, field receipt, optional motion
and ThermalTrial as mutable members. Lines 72-77 compare the accepted field,
accepted thermal state and baseline census, then check thermal owner tickets.
They never hash the candidate field or compare its midpoint with the motion
seal. H/native_joint_binding.hpp:25-30 also exposes the aggregate and admission;
its preflight at 57-59 delegates to that same incomplete check. In the actual
root draft, P/closed_parent_joint_step.hpp:93-117 computes native field/heat
seals once, while 122-139 later publishes the mutable aggregate without
recomputing those seals. N/source/src/mfem_pic_full_time.inc:459-568 validates
and records the supplied seals/endpoints; it does not own the live Hall field.

A concrete source-level counterexample is a valid prepared trial followed by
trial.field_heat->field.E[0] += 1. All tested preflight operands are unchanged;
the modified vector is swapped into accepted state. Changing candidate B,
low words, time or revision has the same gap. Coupler::Trial itself hides its
candidate, but the public ThermalTrial.bodies vector can be replaced by another
valid trial from the same accepted owner/epoch with different Joule input.
That also passes owner preflight without matching the native heat-after seal.
This counterexample was established by the code path; no common commit was run.

Consequence: accepted arena seals and the actual published field/heat can
disagree. A subsequent step may reject, after inconsistency has already been
published. The envelope fix is correct and does not fix this separate defect.

Fix: make the complete joint proposal opaque, move-only and externally const.
At the final root barrier recompute candidate field, candidate heat, source and
motion identities and compare them with the immutable native candidate seals,
including authoritative clocks and actual midpoint arrays. Do not compare only
with an editable copy of FullTimeMotionBinding. Publish those same validated
objects with exclusive ownership and no callback/I/O/allocation between swaps.
Negative mutation and valid-alternate-heat-token tests are required afterward.
V3 Candidate is also public (V/trial_source_binding.hpp:73-81); its preflight is
a field/thermal-identity check, not a seal of candidate content or count truth.
The future common source proposal needs the same complete final binding.

A02 [P1, current admission gap] Initial absolute Gauss law is not established

Evidence: F/axial_fields.hpp:21-101 constructs DC E/B from conductivity and feed
current, without authored particle charge. Fields::start at 131 returns it.
H/axial_joint_v2.hpp:31-48 accepts the census but never compares its deposited
charge with that field. F/axial_fields.hpp:176-181 checks only the field charge
INCREMENT against currents assembled from that same Ampere update.
F/axial_mesh.hpp:97 defines Qfield = -G^T M E. Motion independently verifies
Qparticle_end-Qparticle_begin = dt G^T Jparticle, not initial Qfield=Qphysical.

The isolated probe used the unmodified supported-start field. Its vacuum
nodal charge was at most 1.74069e-25 C. A separately deposited manufactured
-e*1e9 charge had an initial field/charge mismatch of 4.81134e-11 C. This was
only a nodal diagnostic: no native particle or ID was authored or committed.
The current initial-state API has no operand with which to reject that mismatch.
Existing component seeds place equal electron/ion weights at the same positions
(H/test_joint_v2.cpp:14-19), so they do not exercise nonzero initial space charge.

Consequence: increment preservation can propagate an arbitrary initial Gauss
defect. Hashing an authored field and population establishes identity, not
physical consistency. This also applies to restored fields and population.

Fix: root/Hall must admit an explicitly charge-compatible initial state using
the same deposition and boundary/metal charge conventions. Require absolute
Gauss residuals over vacuum plus accounted conductor/short/exterior charge,
and check the actual proposed final native census after both chemistry stages.
Do not silently add compensating background charge or a second Poisson field.
If a constrained initial-field solve is selected, qualify it as an explicit
initialization operation and retain all physical charge-owner operands.

A03 [P1, current admission gap] Non-solenoidal supplied B is accepted

Evidence: F/axial_mesh.hpp:96 provides magnetic_divergence, but
F/axial_fields.hpp:132-197 validates dimensions/finiteness and increment/work
budgets without an initial or candidate divB guard. The incidence tests verify
D curl=0 (F/axial_benchmark.cpp:14-24). That preserves a preexisting divergence
error; it does not make an invalid starting field valid. The current component
loaders likewise do not perform a physical divB admission check.

The field-only probe perturbed one interior B face by 0.01 T. Fields::prepare
accepted divB=50.00000000000004 T/m and returned 50.00000000000005 T/m. Its Gauss
increment was 4.98826e-27 C and energy defect -1.15805e-23 J, both admitted.
The normal DC initial divergence was 1.10845e-12 T/m, consistent with the
operator's floating-point cancellation scale, and is a different quantity.

Fix: validate initial/restored and candidate divergence using the actual high
and low B words, operator scaling and explicit roundoff bounds, and retain a
runtime diagnostic. Preserve the compatible Faraday update. Do not repair an
arbitrary accepted B behind its identity seal or hide its initial residual.

A04 [P2, current exact-binding defect] Motion and Maxwell use different E midpoints

Evidence: H/axial_joint_v2.hpp:44-58 uses .5*(accepted.E+candidate.E), comparing
those high-word midpoint arrays exactly across iterates. In contrast,
F/axial_fields.hpp:149-153 constructs receipt.mid_E as
old.E + (old.E_low + .5*increment/sqrtM), and 166-171 uses that array for plasma
work, Joule heat and circuit/boundary work. The two expressions are not bitwise
equivalent, even when old low words are zero. High/low state is included in
physical field energy, while most force/RHS operations use rounded high words.

The isolated field probe found 9,354 differing edges out of 41,676; maximum
difference was 1.45519e-11 V/m. It compared the actual Maxwell receipt with the
joint port's exact expression; it did not execute a joint nonlinear step.
This is small roundoff in the observed fixture, not evidence of a large energy
error. Nonetheless the current test checks the motion seal against the same
high-word expression (H/test_joint_v2.cpp:49-52), never against receipt.mid_E.
Thus the claim of one identical actual rounded field for all work is too strong.

Fix: expose and use one authoritative immutable midpoint E/B representation
from the field proposal for motion, source sampling, deposition/work and heat.
Define how retained low words enter that representation. Converge on those
exact arrays, or explicitly qualify a separate conversion error if the design
requires one; do not relabel nearby arrays or relax source work bounds.

G01 [P1, full-source integration gate] Source and sink ownership is unfinished

The current closed resident port correctly refuses active IDs and all hits
(H/native_arena_motion.cpp:98,127-139); the native operation requires empty
source/boundary arrays (N/source/src/mfem_pic_full_time.inc:464-467). This is a
safe restriction, not missing removal concealed by an accepted closed step.
It must remain in place until the source/count/RNG/ID/layer and sink owners exist.

V/axial_trial_field.hpp:19-21 uses old axial::push for handoff end-tau, with the
field-domain and old short intersection in F/axial_particles.hpp:46-56. It does
not apply H/kinetic_boundary.cpp's independent native width/gap/short/feed
policy. Widening field air or axial padding can therefore change the handoff
escape location. The canonical ordinary port explicitly rejects any such
kinetic hit. A source extension cannot reuse the padded-field handoff endpoint
or postclip its position while retaining full-flight momentum/current/work.

V3 source paths correctly retain relative displacement before global origin,
already weighted charge/work and common-dt current, including quantum bridges.
Their endpoint impulse contains the surface counterpart of an emitted charge.
The canonical birth/removal and electrode/short/exterior charge bank must be
staged once with that impulse. Collection deposits a path through the incident
endpoint before removing the same ID; the endpoint charge and kinetic energy
need one actual sink owner. Neither a rejected hit nor a staged endpoint is an
accepted escaped/collected charge history. Neutral births also need their own
physical birth-time remaining flight, not ordinary resident full-step motion.

Root/native must also settle collision eligibility and durations for birth,
Active, handoff and recollection. The current parent exposes all resident
electron/H2O carriers for dt/2 at each endpoint. A source-v3 physical birth
time alone does not qualify that schedule for newborns or image-layer electrons.

H/axial_thermal_v2.hpp:45-53 creates no source patches. A new aggregate needs
explicit body/slice/patch origins, normals, height/area partitions and controlled
access to the same accepted Couplers. Prepare Joule plus certified Qwall once
per owner, retaining reference/image/particle changes separately. Finite log
expected count with scalar underflow must survive to the real count owner.
No weight/floor/statistical/birth policy or IDs are supplied by this audit.

The complete requested typed operands and the 13/15 native-dependency comparison
are in inputs/source-v3-review/SOURCE-V3-INTEGRATION-REVIEW.txt. The two native
support differences are observer/transaction plumbing; Boris and kinetic-energy
functions match byte-for-byte, but API-v3.txt's exact header binding still needs
an explicit owner update. Do not link its test support as a second native arena.

A05 [P2, runtime provenance gap] Drive and boundary forcing are not common-state seals

H/axial_thermal_v2.hpp:10-27 parses any well-formed 12-column trace with the
expected labels and finite increasing knots. It enforces support but not the
declared trace SHA256. H/axial_joint_v2.hpp:9-20 seals field state and the thermal
material/state, not the C19 curve, interpolation/clock registration, initial
DC scenario or boundary-force vectors. F/axial_fields.hpp:129 exposes Initial
publicly; its feed/background_force are used on every prepare. The root draft
checks field/heat/source/mesh/material but not that actual drive/operator setup.

The reviewed C19 copy DOES match its source and declared hash:
2c54d2dbaec6b6c8fcf2e45b5abd837aa72999f5fd14217f530277ffd5949076.
This is a restart/runtime binding gap, not evidence that a different trace was
used in the preserved component run. Fix by sealing the actual curve and its
role, clock mapping, geometry/operator and boundary/initialization scenario in
the common configuration, validating on restart, and making them immutable.
Source-code identity alone cannot authenticate a separately read runtime CSV.

G02 [P2, common-restart gate] Component replay is not a complete atomic checkpoint

H/axial_thermal_v2.hpp:123-133 now loads all thermal slices into private owners
and swaps only after their clocks/files validate. That is a useful atomic
aggregate improvement. Its caller must still validate field/native/source/RNG
identities before exposing any of those candidate owners. The test fixture
H/test_thermal_v2.cpp:22-27 loads thermal state before checking the field/thermal
clock match; a mismatch can throw after that local thermal owner has changed.
Do not promote that fixture loader into the common restore path. The old v1
F/axial_restart.hpp also predates the canonical native arena/source/RNG design.

Native full-time storage and ParentSubflow checkpoint their own state and
bindings. Neither supplies the missing root publication of all components.
Fresh-process byte parity of 9 thermal-v2 fixture files is not crash consistency
of a common step. Root needs private candidate construction for every owner,
complete physical and identity checks, and one durable checkpoint publication
protocol with failure tests. A rename alone is not a demonstrated power-loss
durability guarantee. No crash-consistency experiment was run in this audit.

Other audited behavior, limitations and evidence

Discrete field constraints: the SI Yee incidence complex retains x/y/z
derivatives; compatible gradient/curl adjoints and D curl identity are tested.
Gauss increment plus charge-conserving particle paths are useful ongoing
checks, but absolute total charge requires the initial and owner checks in A02.
Native reactions preserve charge at the primary and target anchors or reject
nonlocal transfer (N/source/src/mfem_pic_arena.cpp:881-926). Ionization products
are not allowed to relocate charge without a compatible current path.

Domains: EM uses a centered finite air box and axial padding (F/axial_mesh.hpp).
Kinetics uses native (0..width,0..gap,short..feed) and an explicit translation
(-width/2,-gap/2,0), with unrotated E/B/u (H/native_arena_motion.cpp:102).
P/native_parent_subflow.hpp:35-91 builds a separate physical collision grid,
excludes aligned solid cells, uses actual 3D vacuum volume and rejects cut foils
or ambiguous material-surface locations. Its grid/configuration is bound into
the parent RNG state identity at 155-163; it is not the EM air-box cell volume.
Changing EM width must not enlarge kinetic or collision volume. Source global
origins need the inverse translation once on native admission. Actual proposed
boundary geometry still needs G01's owner port and qualification.

C19 interpretation and thesis evidence: feed_shape is a normalized impressed
upper-to-lower branch at z=+12.5 mm. Terminal voltage is -feed dot E, positive
lower-minus-upper, and circuit work is I*V*dt. Conduction, plasma, displacement
and outer terms respond to the field solve; no second gap voltage is prescribed.
The negative-z PEC short has zero modeled heating; its initial return profile
is a reflected feed quadrature, not a recovered experimental lead geometry.
The outer admittance dissipates Y E_mid^2 while a static background_force
supplies its separately reported work (F/axial_fields.hpp:94-100,160-171).
Thus circuit input alone is not the entire external energy ledger.

D/shot_17133_current.json identifies C.19 as recovered published average-current
display vertices, not raw independent samples. Literal support is
0.749788055492..196.74963671 ns. Trigger registration is unknown; digitization
envelopes are not physical confidence bounds. The Hall start is a declared
300 K DC state at the first supported knot, not recovered prepulse history.
D/figure_4_28_simulation_drive.json and figure_4_29_simulation_drive.json are
separate legacy Allegra raster drives at 40/60 ns, with unknown clock relation
and original arrays. Figure 4.30 metadata flags unresolved legend units.
Those comparisons cannot be silently aligned, renormalized or treated as
validated heating inputs. This audit inspected frozen recovery metadata and
checked the C19 CSV hash; it did not reparse or redigitize the thesis PDF.

The Hall evidence covers early sub-ps operator fixtures, not those thesis
histories. The earlier 9/17/25 mm box comparison leaves a 5.57% central-B change
from 17 to 25 mm; the small uniform exterior is explicitly unconverged. The
wide graded exterior, axial refinement, actual lead/short geometry and a
registered observable comparison remain physical gates. There is no evidence
here establishing experimental heating, breakdown or an axial instability.

Thermal timing: conductivity is sampled once from accepted T_n
(H/axial_joint_v2.hpp:41), held throughout the EM iteration, then the actual
midpoint Joule cell power enters one v2 heat solve per body/slice. Height dz is
used once; conduction edge and cell Joule quadratures agree. Accepted T_{n+1}
changes the next step's conductivity. This is lagged electrothermal coupling,
not generally second order with strong thermal feedback; the heat owner uses
backward Euler. Tiny-heating EM refinement does not establish nonlinear thermal
accuracy. Source-v3 explicitly holds accepted temperature while E/B is a
trial midpoint; that is truthful staging, not midpoint thermal temperature.
The evaluated-solid 298.2..1500 K, rho_ref=8000, fixed-volume Cp-as-Cv model,
no axial thermal diffusion, no short heat, mechanics or vapor remain limits.

Orbit/work/current: F/foil_particles.hpp:40-43 calls unchanged pic_boris for
proper momentum. Its displacement is conjugate to the two electric half-kick
energy increments; magnetic rotation does no kinetic work. It is a distinct
full-time orbit map, not the old native D-K-D phase. Axial Path uses the same
piecewise polynomial basis for gathering and qD/dt deposition, and endpoints
for continuity. No extra Hall drift, rotated B or post-motion energy repair
was found. Native-only momentum equality held in all eight isolated probes at
proper-velocity scales 0, 0.1, 1 and 10 c, with electric and magnetic cases.
Maximum observed single-particle work defect was 9.96e-30 J, within explicit
operand roundoff; represented displacement speeds remained below c. This is
not a new orbit/time/spatial convergence qualification or boundary test.
Ordinary motion still admits its documented 1e-20 J absolute work floor;
source-v3's stricter actual-operand checks cannot be replaced with that floor.
The full-common dt, gyro<=0.05, displacement<=0.2 cell and plasma<=0.1 guards
remain unchanged. Current is an edge load in A m, not a raw velocity moment.

Common publication: parent staged scheduler/product RNGs and exact private
exposure identities are checked against the native token
(P/native_parent_subflow.hpp:440-485). Native motion checks the true provider
record seal, exact census/endpoints/species/weights and closed coverage. These
are positive identity safeguards. The root then publishes native, parent and
field/heat with nonthrowing swaps, but A01 currently invalidates the assertion
that every published object was covered by the prior common barrier.

Next action is root's consolidation and explicit separately versioned remediation
instructions. G01/G02 stay closed until their interfaces are real. Corrections
will need focused negative/admission checks against new snapshots before a full
coupled qualification. No full integration run was launched by this audit.
