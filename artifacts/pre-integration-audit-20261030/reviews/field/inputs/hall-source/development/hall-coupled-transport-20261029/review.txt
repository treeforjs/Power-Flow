Measured results

Signed uniform-field control, n_e=n_i=1e8 cm^-3, Bz=+10 T, Ex=1e5 V/m,
physical proton/electron mass ratio; 20 electron gyroperiods:
  Electron mean Jy: 0.160217921788 A/m^2
  Ion mean Jy:      -0.000125043057445 A/m^2
  Total mean Jy:    0.160092878731 A/m^2
  Fine normalized current error: 1.6127324e-06
B reversal reverses the currents. Time refinement is approximately second order.
A balanced common E cross B drift gives zero relative current. These results
measure transient kinetic relative transport; no added Hall velocity is used.

Self-consistent native Poisson control, n_e=n_i=3.14207783e+14 cm^-3,
Bx=5.68563011 T, manufactured ion mass ratio 25:
  Mesh-refined potential L2 error at 64 cells: 0.00060639804
  Max electron displacement error / initial amplitude: 0.00073468109
  Max ion displacement error / (initial amplitude / 25): 0.0028981129
  Max relative energy defect at dt=2.5 fs: 8.4524512e-07
  Max accumulated absolute kick-work defect / initial field energy:
    1.916408e-13
  Max species continuity defect / gross charge over all native cases:
    4.8133942e-17
  Max Gauss defect / gross charge:
    1.6298988e-17
Potential, electron and ion waveform errors show approximately second-order
mesh convergence. Energy error scales as dt^2. Sampling at fixed physical density
converges toward the fixed-grid result; increasing sampling alone does not remove
Q1 mesh error. A separate physical proton-mass case also advances both species.
The tested restart continuation is bitwise identical for positions, proper
velocities and accumulated work. Wrong-species and corrupt-file restarts fail.

An invalid second member of a source batch rolls back; IDs and valid cached
fields remain unchanged. A successful collocated authored electron/positive-ion
pair deposits together and creates zero initial net field. Positive/negative
wall charge signs and kinetic throughput also pass. These are authored source
transactions, not simulated ionization events or water secondaries.

Cold two-fluid Maxwell control with physical proton/electron mass ratio:
  Continuum mode omega/omega_pe at k*de=0.2: 0.0193067065099
  Finest (128 samples/wavelength) frequency relative error: 0.00018890678
  Max relative field+species energy defect across coupled cases:
    9.5759416e-12
  Mesh and time convergence orders are approximately two.
Disabling current feedback changes omega/omega_pe from approximately 0.01929 to
0.20013 and produces a large unbalanced energy gain. Disabling Faraday also
changes the mode and breaks the closed energy balance. The Hall-MHD frequency
limit differs from full cold Maxwell by 4.98% at k*de=0.2 and 0.140% at k*de=0.025.
The finite-electron-inertia/displacement-current limit must be stated explicitly.
This is a Fourier representation of a periodic staggered-curl linear mode. Its
identically zero divJ cannot qualify general electromagnetic particle deposition.

Cost and resource scope

The final benchmark used 3.408 s inside frozen userspace.
Build, benchmark, original foundation tests, validation and plotting used one
CPU6 job at nice15 with one thread under the shared testing-core.lock lease.
No numerical acceptance limit was relaxed. Every populated PIC timestep retained
the 10 fs cap and gyro/plasma/displacement checks. Dependencies were unchanged.
No GPU execution, production run, scheduled monitor or heating-field adoption
occurred. The frozen long PIC process and cgroup were not touched.

Remaining boundary

The actual mapping still lacks a coupled vector electromagnetic field state,
compatible all-component current deposition (including Jz), field-work-adjoint
interpolation, electromagnetic boundaries/circuit ports and an EM restart schema.
Its prescribed native B plus 2D Poisson self field cannot represent general
Hall-MHD induction or the resolved-drift resistive instability in the 2026 paper.
next-integration.txt specifies the patch sequence and matched benchmark gates.
Full H2O rates, exclusive branching, secondary laws and recoil remain unsupported
where the existing data registry marks them missing. This prototype introduces
no guessed rates and no instantaneous fragmentation closure.
