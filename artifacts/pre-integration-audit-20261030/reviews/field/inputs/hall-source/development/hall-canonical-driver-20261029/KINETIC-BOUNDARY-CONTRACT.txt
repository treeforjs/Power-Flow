Explicit finite-target kinetic boundary contract, version 1

The selected kinetic policy retains the sealed canonical v6.2 finite-target
classification from GapPic::drift_until_absorbed. It does not derive an escape
plane from the Maxwell mesh, its transverse extent, or its axial air cells.

Native coordinates are x in [0,width_m], y in [0,gap_m], and z in the declared
[short_z_m,feed_z_m]. width/gap here are the independently authored KINETIC box.
All outer xy faces absorb/escape. Foils are the native lower/upper rectangles
extruded through this target interval. At short_z, only x within the lower foil
width and y between lower.top and upper.bottom is the short bridge. The rest of
that plane is axial escape. The whole feed_z plane is axial escape. No reflection
or extended axial streaming through electric-field air padding is introduced.

First-hit selection follows the original order: outer x, outer y, lower foil,
upper foil, then target-end plane. A later candidate wins an exactly equal
fraction. No epsilon creates, postpones, or advances a hit. Entry is evaluated
on the integrator's represented straight segment; fraction 1 is a boundary hit.
The unit normal points OUT OF VACUUM (into metal or the external sink). Foil
corner patch ownership still belongs to the source/boundary owner.

kinetic_boundary.hpp declares the policy, its independent SHA256, a segment
first-hit helper and a neutral full-time stream proposal. The neutral proposal
uses x(t0), u(t0), authoritative dt, relativistic velocity u/gamma, and unchanged
u. It reports the first-hit native endpoint, flight and remaining duration,
sink classification, native ID, exact carrier weight and incident kinetic energy.
It does not remove a carrier, change Count2, allocate an ID, move any clock,
attribute wall heat, invoke Poisson or run D-K-D. Canonical owner must admit all
carriers of a packet consistently and own the kinetic/internal/reference sink
receipt. No sink policy can be inferred from a motion endpoint alone.

Motion receipt v2 requires kinetic_policy_sha256 separately
from mesh_sha256 and contains the explicit policy description. A centered native
to field translation is applied once, using the KINETIC width/gap, even when the
field box is wider. The ordinary no-source motion port continues to reject hits;
the neutral helper makes first-hit proposals available for the native owner's
later typed sink admission. This avoids silently claiming boundary publication
or source heat integration. Charged boundary admission still requires a coupled
partial-flight/work/source operation; the no-boundary port rejects it.

Qualification requires fixed native policy with two different field exteriors,
neutral and charged xy/feed escapes, short bridge versus nonbridge escape,
foil hits, exact endpoint-time hits, neutral remaining time and kinetic energy,
unchanged identity/weight/momentum, one native-to-field translation, and source
ownership rejection. Widening only the field mesh must not change first-hit
kind, native endpoint, flight, policy hash, or accepted no-hit force/trajectory.
