Electron energy port
Transfer.certified_source_exchange selects ONLY these weighted integrated J:
 source_wall_J

The certified equation is Qwall + dK + dRef_e + dImage = Wfield, with source-owner
operand-scaled1e-10 arithmetic bound and no absolute energy floor. All legacy
kinetic/reference/Nottingham/collision/authored fields must be zero in this mode.

field/current owner. No field work is relabeled as Joule or material reference.
Budget and cumulative indices14..18 expose these five terms in the listed order.
Signed electron reference and image energy remain distinct from water chemistry.

is a diagnostic of the adsorbed reservoir change, NEVER a transported gas reference
or fragment debit. Negative adsorbed reference is
 Uads(N)=-Eb0*q_e*[N-alpha_ref*N*N/(2*N_ML)].
Removing actual dN raises it by
