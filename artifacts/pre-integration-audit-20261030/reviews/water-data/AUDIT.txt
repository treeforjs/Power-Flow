with conditional equal-pair kinematics, not NIST's detailed BED spectrum.
The H2Oplus recombination source lead has no admitted numerical rate/kernel.

The original NIST water model represents eight valence electrons. Its10keV
formula ceiling and generic accuracy statement to about1keV do not establish
all-shell or full-tail accuracy. At8084.470604111418eV,73% of its2a1 term
remains unmapped; even the mapped fraction of the four-valence model is not
physical total-ionization coverage. Exact rates and the limited denominator
are recorded in audit.json. No missing rate was redistributed.

The new Auger branches are a conditional adaptation of Montenegro2015's
high-velocity fragmentation matrix, not energy-resolved measured electron
branching across1-10keV. A fixed36.88eV hole leaves5.16456eV for OHplus/Hplus
and only0.163505eV for Oplus/Hplus/H plus Auger kinetic energy. These budgets
and phase-space sharing are not measured KER/Auger spectra. Exact numerical
energy/momentum closure cannot establish their physical distributions.

Angular provenance remains explicit. Ram2010 provides the near6.5eV Hminus
lab-angle/vibration anchor. NIST SRD64 v5 supplies20 H/O atomic exports on
1keV energy and1degree angle grids. Their piecewise-mu DCS integral defines
the IAM rate; Muñoz molecular elastic data are diagnostic, not a rescaling.
The1.9058% maximum discrepancy from rounded atomic totals is a numerical
mesh check, not molecular accuracy. Molecular interference/bonding/dipole
rotation and complete sub-keV angles are missing. No extrapolation fills
SongTable2's angular/energy gaps.

RGA background cannot initialize a calibrated mixture. The corrected record
has50 nominal channels;450 PDF drawing rectangles are graphics provenance.
Ionizer fragments and overlapping species prevent identifying neutral
O/OH/H/H2O fractions from their mass peaks alone. The thesis page44/printed19
was inspected in full. Instrument identity/settings, cracking/sensitivity
matrix, raw timing and independent gauge/gas-temperature information remain
missing. The approximate5e-5Torr, ALL-H2O,300K density1.6094e12cm^-3 is a
named conditional scenario, not measured composition or surface coverage.
The SRS manufacturer source supports this measurement distinction; it does
not identify the actual Mykonos instrument. Superseded recovery directories
are not used as independent measurements.
