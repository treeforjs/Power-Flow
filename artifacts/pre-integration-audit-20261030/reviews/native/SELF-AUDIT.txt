Parent-requested remediation interface and exact history ceiling
  After audit, a NEW corrective API candidate should expose immutable
  PreparedArena::candidate_full_time_boundary(). Root can compare the actual
  candidate native field/heat/source seals, time and revision against real
  provider tokens during the single common preflight. It is not present in001
  or002, and neither archive is changed. Making the root joint proposal opaque
  is a separate necessary owner fix; accepting JSON hashes alone does not bind
  a subsequently mutable field/heat proposal.

  Even an empty full-time outer step writes FIVE journal entries: begin, C1,
  motion, C2, boundary. With maximum_journal_entries=4096, an absolute ideal
  ceiling is floor(4096/5)=819 accepted empty steps (8.19 ps at10 fs). Declared
  future reaction budget headroom, any actual events and the16MiB byte bound
  reject earlier. This is thousands of times too short for60ns: 60ns/10fs is
  6,000,000 steps. Full in-memory history copies and repeated full-prefix dumps
  add substantial growth cost before the cap. No production timing/size claim.
  A qualified bounded checkpoint-anchor/rolling commitment plus external
  append-only receipts is required before a long run, preserving restart local
  conservation/phase/owner semantics. Merely increasing caps or silently dropping
  records is not a correction.
