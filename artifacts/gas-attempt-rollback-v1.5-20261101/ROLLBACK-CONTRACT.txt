Gas v1.5: exact-attempt rollback for common coordinator trials
============================================================

begin_attempt(step) opens a step and returns an opaque, move-only rollback
capability bound to owner lifetime, begin generation and step. Unlike the
read/content CandidateBinding, it deliberately survives candidate mutations,
failure and publication preparation. It carries no content-read or publication
authority. matches_attempt and abandon_attempt are noexcept and allocate
nothing. A foreign/default/moved/destroyed/published/retried token cannot
abandon a live candidate. A refused abandon leaves its token untouched.

If begin_step fails after its own generation advanced, begin_attempt cleans up
that new candidate before rethrowing. Failure to begin on an already open
owner does not discard the existing candidate. The legacy explicit begin_step
API is unchanged. Owner::abandon and DeviceLane::abandon now declare their
existing nonthrowing cleanup contract. Device abandon launches no work.

The maintained integration helper lives in Power-Flow/native/coupled_pic.
GasStepScope retains shared owner lifetime and performs exact-attempt cleanup
on unwind/reset. Move assignment first abandons the previous scope. Successful
publication invalidates the attempt, so old scope destruction cannot affect a
new trial. Serial owner use is required. It does not replace other owners'
rollback guards or the required joint preflight/no-fail publication window.

No numerical, configuration, checkpoint or physics policy changes. Actual
state/motion/publication/binding histories are compared with sealed v1.4
evidence; scope-controlled populated history is also compared to the explicit
path and checkpoint continuation. Scope tests include key-capacity poisoning,
allocation failure, same-digest retry and failure before/after actual device
begin copy. Injected device exceptions do not qualify arbitrary driver recovery.

The source, frozen execution, exact job outputs, CPU/sanitizer/device results
and unchanged-state comparison are recorded by preserve-rollback.py. Failed
initial build424 was an intentional self-move test triggering -Werror; its
aliasing test form was corrected without changing the helper or compiler gate.
No speedup, common-owner closure or physical breakdown qualification is claimed.
