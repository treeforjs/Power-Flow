SS304 phase thermal owner v1.2 -- integration contract (2026-10-31)

Base contracts: INTEGRATION-v1.1.txt and INTEGRATION-v1.txt (namespace ss304_phase_v1_2 here, per-cell functions
px_*). Below is what changes for Hall, the native owner and the source owner when they adopt v1.2. Nothing is
integrated here.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Hall

1.1 Construction: Config::enable_v1_2(c) = enable_v1_1 + depth-averaged melt layer (flow_model 2) + ejection +
    diagnostics. The slices of one foil body must carry consecutive k (SliceSpec::k) for flow along the 25 mm height;
    the slice map of the Hall foil (125 k x 2 bodies) is already of that form. GpuOptions::session = &hall_session
    as in v1.1.
1.2 Sub-cycling (recommended). Per field step, after Hall has the cell power and J, B on the device:
        owner.accumulate_device(d_power, d_JB, dt_field);      // d_power[n_em] W, d_JB[6 n_em] (J A/m2, B T)
    (device pointers on the shared session, read in stream order; no synchronization, one small kernel), or
    owner.accumulate(power, JB, dt_field) with host vectors. Every K field steps:
        StepInput in; in.use_accumulated = true;               // dt = window (in.dt 0 or the window length)
        (loads, deposits, back pressures, releases as before) owner.prepare(in) / commit, or owner.advance(in)
        sigma = owner.sigma_em();                              // from the step, no extra evaluation
    and use sigma for the next K field steps. Joule: the window word is the exact sum of the per-field-step words
    from_double(P_e dt_k), i.e. the ledger is the same as calling the owner every field step. Melt force: the window
    mean of (J x B) dt per EM cell.
    Accuracy bound per window (StepRecord): sigma_change_max = max over EM cells |sigma_end/sigma_start - 1| (the
    error of using sigma_start for K field steps; choose K so that it stays below the field tolerance, e.g. 1e-3);
    window_heating_K = max over EM cells of the window Joule energy / (m c_p,min) (largest possible temperature
    change inside one window). Measured on the SS304 foil test (3e6 W per EM cell): K = 16 gives sigma change
    5.2e-5 and a temperature difference to the per-field-step owner of 7e-5 K (bound 0.79 K).
1.3 Energy exchange term Hall must book. The owner books W_L = StepRecord::lorentz (exact 2^-96 J word, also per
    patch: StepRecord::patch_lorentz[p], patch -> slice and face) as energy the melt received from the
    electromagnetic field during the window: W_L = integral of u . (J x B) dV dt (tangential force on the
    depth-averaged velocity plus the inward normal load on the normal motion of the layer). Hall's Ohm's law is that
    of a conductor at rest (J = sigma E), so its field-energy balance books only J^2/sigma (the Joule column already
    handed to the owner). For a moving conductor J . E = J^2/sigma + u . (J x B); Hall must therefore debit its
    field-energy ledger by W_L each window:
        Delta W_field = - Joule - W_L - (Poynting out of the domain) + (sources)
    so that field + owner (thermal + kinetic) close. W_L < 0 means the melt moved against the force and the field
    gained energy. Not a Hall term: StepRecord::surface (W_S, recoil and capillary/Marangoni work: vapor momentum and
    surface energy) and StepRecord::dissipation (internal kinetic -> thermal conversion inside the owner).
    Not done (A2): the back-EMF u x B in Hall's Ohm's law; the owner can report the layer velocity per patch
    (PatchReport::layer_ux_m_s, layer_uz_m_s) if Hall adds it.
1.4 Owner energy closure (every step, exact): dE + dK = Joule + loads + metal_return + boundary + W_L + W_S
    - evaporation(reference + kinetic + internal) - radiation - release_energy - ejected_energy (record fields dE,
    dK, joule, loads, deposit_E, boundary, lorentz, surface, evap_ref, evap_kin, evap_int, radiation, release_E,
    eject_E). Atoms: dN_i = deposited - evaporated - released - ejected (eject_N).
1.5 Refusals added: melt-flow-cfl (a layer wave would still cross more than cfl of a column after the sub-step
    doublings: shorten the window), invalid-input for a nonfinite or negative accumulated input (reported at the
    owner step), checkpoint with a non-empty window. geometry-departure now concerns mainly crater depth: outward
    displacement above 0.25 EM cell is ejected (1.6/2.1); the refusal stays at 0.5. melt-flow-reynolds applies to the
    lubrication model only.
1.6 Digest: state_sha256 hashes the cell and face-column roots (v1.1 tags) plus the layer-flow root
    ("ss304-phase-v1.2/layer-flow": momentum, layer mass, kinetic-energy word per face column) and the new ledger
    words; leaves on the device as in v1.1.
1.7 Cost on the Hall foil (262,500 cells, RTX 3080 Ti, all v1.2 options, 10 fs field step): owner step 3.0 ms per
    window (one sub-step up to K = 746), accumulation 1.5 us per field step; per field step 3.0 / 0.75 / 0.38 / 0.19 /
    0.093 ms for K = 1 / 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 (RESULTS.txt Sec. 3, with the device time per kernel group). Suggested
    start: K = 8 (0.38 ms next to Hall's 2.85 ms field step), raised or lowered from sigma_change_max.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Native owner (ejected melt)
2.1 Output per owner step: StepRecord::eject_N[6] (atoms Fe, Cr, Ni, Mn, Si, C), eject_E (J: thermal energy of the
    liquid in the owner's enthalpy scale plus kinetic energy), eject_K (J: kinetic part); per patch:
    patch_eject_N[p], patch_eject_E[p], patch_eject_K[p]; cumulative per patch in PatchReport::cumulative_ejected_
    atoms / _J / _kinetic_J. Thermal part E_th = eject_E - eject_K; mean specific enthalpy (E_th / mass) gives the
    droplet temperature through the owner's law (px_T_of_h); mean speed sqrt(2 eject_K / mass).
2.2 Origin: open x edges (liquid leaving a face at x = x0 or x0 + width, moving along +-x in the face plane) and
    departure (liquid lifted beyond 0.25 EM cell, leaving along the face normal). The owner does not resolve droplet
    sizes or directions beyond this.
2.3 The ejected mass and energy are no longer in the owner. A future droplet/vapor species of the native owner must
    take them (as macro-particles or a reservoir) to keep the coupled ledger closed; until then the coupled ledger
    must book them as a sink column "ejected melt" with the owner's words.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Source owner: unchanged from INTEGRATION-v1.1.txt Sec. 2 (back pressure per patch, surface composition, recoil).
   The recoil pressure now drives the inertial layer (PatchReport::recoil_pressure_Pa unchanged).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Fixed Hall mesh (update of INTEGRATION-v1.1.txt Sec. 4)
4.1 Outward displacement is limited by ejection at Config::eject_fraction (0.25 EM cell); inward displacement (crater
    from evaporation, ejection and outflow) still ends in the geometry refusal at 0.5 EM cell
    (StepRecord::max_crater_fraction, PatchReport::crater_depth_m report it).
4.2 Item 4.2e of v1.1 (melt leaving the foil) is now the ejected-melt reservoir (2.1); items a-d and f remain.
