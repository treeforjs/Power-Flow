A19 -- convergence and validation plan for the coupled PIC water-breakdown run (draft, 2026-10-31)
Status of A19: NOT STARTED (no joint, pilot-to-breakdown or convergence run exists). This plan assumes the integration of
INTEGRATION.txt (Hall v3.3 port-current drive, coordinator v8 circuit) and the open Hall prerequisites below.
Tags: every threshold here is "recommended" (proposed, not measured); data items refer to INVENTORY.txt ids.

0. Prerequisites (blocking)
 P1 Hall vacuum refusal at 1.236 ns (absolute Gauss allowance fixed at construction; Hall v3.1 README 7) resolved.
 P2 Port-current drive in Hall v3.3 and the circuit in coordinator v8; prescribed-C19 regression (tests R1/R2) kept.
 P3 Transverse box: the 9 mm box gives 11.05 nH at the port vs 16.15 nH at 45.4 mm (box study, 200 um cells); a
    voltage or breakdown comparison needs the converged exterior (>= 35 mm, or a graded exterior if Hall gains one).
 P4 Clock registration: the circuit fits place the plotted origin at -0.2 to -15.5 ns (17133)
    (15210: +0.1 to +15.3 ns) after switch closure depending on the inductance variant; timing comparisons inherit this spread until a trigger
    reference is supplied (A17).
 P5 Inductance question resolved (README 6): with 144 nF / 800 kV the measured rise needs ~47-52 nH in total, which the
    supplied 36 nH LTD + 40 nH feed + target cannot give. The coupled run uses the set chosen after that answer
    (C3 meets the current and 220 kV targets with the 45 mm box but sets the feed to ~0).

1. Runs
 R-vac  Vacuum pre-run with the circuit from switch closure (I = 0, E = B = 0) to T_on (20-40 ns circuit time), then
        native genesis bound to that Hall state (pilot limitation L8). Cost now: 24 ms/step at 10 fs (GPU) ->
        2e6-4e6 steps, 13-27 h; with dt_max raised for vacuum steps (implicit field; v3.1 dt_max configurable to 1 ns,
        but the dt guard and the step-controller receipts must admit it) 100 fs-1 ps gives 0.4-4 h.
 R-mesh Transverse cell 200 / 100 / 50 / 25 um (9 mm box: 45/90/180/360 cells; 45.4 mm box: 227/454/908/1816) with
        axial foil cells 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 (now 6.25 mm; the axial voltage drop and the feed-to-short heating gradient
        are unresolved at 4). Same circuit set, same w0, same dt, same seeds. If the 45 mm box is too large at 25 um,
        run the series in the 9 mm box and add the box correction measured at 200 and 100 um (the exterior acts as a
        separable series inductance at the port; verify at two cell sizes).
        Debye length 7434 sqrt(T_e[eV]/n[m^-3]) m: at 3 eV 129 um (1e16), 41 um (1e17), 13 um (1e18). 25 um cells resolve
        it to ~2.6e17 m^-3 at 3 eV (cell <= lambda_D) or ~1e18 (cell <= 2 lambda_D); statements about screening above
        that density are not supported by this series.
 R-part Peak macroparticle count 1e4 / 1e5 / 1e6 per species, realized by the stream weight w0 (now 1e6 for every
        stream; no merge/split exists, pilot L6): w0 = N_phys,peak / N_target from the previous level's peak count.
        Seeds: 5 at 1e4, 5 at 1e5, 2 at 1e6 (the avalanche onset is stochastic: separate seed variance from bias).
 R-dt   dt 20 / 10 / 5 / 2.5 fs over a 1 ns window around the first avalanche and over the breakdown time, finest mesh
        that is affordable, identical seeds. Controller receipts already published by Hall v3.1: omega_p dt (1e18 m^-3
        -> 5.6e-4 at 10 fs; 1e22 -> 0.056), omega_c dt (100 T -> 0.18 at 10 fs; Boris phase error (omega_c dt)^2/12
        ~ 0.3 %), max displacement in cells, drive change per step. Choose the largest dt whose metric changes are
        below one third of the finest-mesh discretization error, with omega_c dt <= 0.2 and displacement <= 0.5 cell.
        The circuit itself is second order and dt-insensitive at these steps (test T2).

2. Convergence metrics and acceptance (recommended)
 Observables Q: port current at t_bd + 20 ns; target-entrance voltage peak; breakdown time t_bd (simulated
 resistive-voltage collapse to < 20 % of its pre-collapse maximum); ionization events up to t_bd + 5 ns; peak and
 gap-averaged n_e at t_bd + 5 ns; foil edge surface temperature at 40 ns; desorbed H2O count at 40 ns; Hall midgap B_x
 at 40/60 ns (code-to-code).
 Mesh: observed order p from the three finest levels (Richardson); fine-grid GCI (safety 1.25) <= 5 % for current and
   voltage, <= 10 % for t_bd and plasma quantities; monotone convergence required, otherwise the level is reported as
   not converged.
 Particles: |Q(1e6) - Q(1e5)| <= 2 sigma_seed(1e5); seed spread at 1e6 <= 3 % (t_bd within 1 ns).
 dt: |Q(dt) - Q(dt/2)| <= GCI_mesh / 3.
 Ledgers at every step of every run: charge (exact), Gauss and divB within their bounds, Hall field energy within its
 admitted 1e-10 activity bound, circuit ledger within its summed rounding bound, join (port iteration defect + Hall dot
 rounding) <= 1e-12 of the delivered energy (tests H1/H2: <= 1.7e-14 J over 1,000-2,000 steps, <= 1e-13 relative).

      peak, 36/37 % rise.

 M-E4 circuit rating vs E9: FWHM into 0.5 ohm within 10 % of 160 ns (circuit only; sets A, B, D within 4.3 %, C3 +8 %).
 M-T1 breakdown time vs E4 (15210 collapse ~52-60 ns plotted) and T1: within 5 ns after clock registration (P4);
      until P4 is closed report t_bd on the circuit clock and the plotted-time interval implied by the offset spread.

      within 5 ns, with the same registration caveat.

 M-F1 code-to-code (M1/M2): Hall midgap B_x vs Allegra at 40/60 ns within 15 % after P3 (12-14 % low with the 9 mm box);
      informational (legacy units unverified).
