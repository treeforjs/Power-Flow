This is a primary-source inventory, not a reaction-table release. No active
runtime, native dependency, guard, scientific tolerance or production binding
changed. Native v8.4 remains bound to sealed chemistry v3.1.1. The comparison
table here is the separate prepared v3.2.2, identified in BASE-PINS.json.

The three encounter groups e+OH, e+O and e+H2 are absent from that table, despite
those species existing in its registry. They are separate from its 135 refusing
regions inside existing groups. The strict scope guard must remain enabled:
adding one reaction to a group does not establish that the group is complete.

Local review covered v3.2.2's frozen sources/extracted inputs, the earlier
h2o-collision-data runtime, and Raikou's mechanism/provenance descriptions.
No already-qualified numerical dataset for these three neutral targets was
found in that scoped inventory. H2O dissociation labels mentioning OH, O or H2
identify products, not those species as collision targets. The earlier
fragment-continuation-gaps-v8.json also records these continuation gaps.

Primary sources and concrete import constraints

H2: downloaded two original MCCC X(1Sigma_g+), v=0 tables. Total ionization has
21 rows, 15.4327-1000 eV, in a0^2; it explicitly combines dissociative and
non-dissociative ionization. The b(3Sigma_u+) dissociative-excitation file has
54 rows, 4.47713-150 eV, in a0^2. These are calculated data, not measurements.
Exact filenames, hashes and retrieval selectors are in SOURCE-PINS.json.
The papers provide state-resolved context:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adt.2020.101361
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adt.2023.101573
https://www.mccc-db.org/
The site prohibits third-party redistribution and commercial use. Raw files
are retained only in the separate local reference directory, not this artifact.

OH: Chakrabarti, Laporta and Tennyson (2019) give fixed-geometry R-matrix
elastic and excitation results; inspected figures plot elastic 1-10 eV and
excitation up to 10 eV in bohr^2. Table 1 calculated vertical excitations are
4.05, 7.36 and 7.89 eV. Dissociation and DEA are explicitly approximate, and
the quoted dissociation D0=3.88 eV differs from this registry's 4.411033 eV
O+H reference-energy cost. A machine-readable channel table was not acquired.
Do not turn figure axes into a certified table domain or reuse H2O rates.
https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6595/ab364c
https://arxiv.org/html/1907.08701v1
The arXiv version grants distribution rights to arXiv, not a general open-data
license. The PDF stays local. Sobrinho et al. (2004) separately provide a
1-500 eV theoretical elastic/DCS/MT and total-absorption lead, but absorption
does not resolve ionization versus dissociation products:
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.70.032717

O: acquired the atomic-O supplement to Song et al. (2026), distinct from its
O2 supplement. The workbook has total, ES, MT, five excitation, DCS and
ionization sheets. Its CC BY-NC 4.0 license differs from the manuscript's
CC BY license. Actual sheet extents are recorded separately; they are not
automatically the recommended theoretical domain. The DCS sheet has 31
energies from 0.014 to 9.999 eV and a header literally saying
"DCS(10^16 cm^2/sr)". Resolve that exponent before using it. ES/MT state
10^-16 cm^2 and extend about 0.069389-260.005 eV. ION spans 13.62-5000 eV.
Excitation first-zero rows are calculated onsets, not independently verified
physical thresholds. Source bytes are unchanged.
https://doi.org/10.60893/figshare.jpr.30850010.v1
https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0287254
The original BSR paper covers elastic, excitation and ionization through
200 eV, with ground and metastable states distinguished. Its available
full data were offered by the authors; the new supplement is not an implicit
license to extend every channel or omit unlisted transitions.
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.94.042707

Recommended minimal next increment

Start a separately versioned H2(X,v=0) b(3Sigma_u+) dissociation rate component
using the exact 54-row MCCC file. This has a specific target and dissociative
channel, a finite tabulated interval, and products that can be represented
as two H atoms plus the scattered electron once the state/asymptotic and
event-law contract is qualified. First deliver the importer, provenance,
unit conversion, interpolation tests and strict coverage rows. Do not enable
production events merely because that scalar rate imports successfully.

Before event admission, reconcile the source onset with the registry reference
cost (4.478069261841885 eV for 2H-H2), including finite target mass. Preserve
the source table; do not silently shift its energies, lower the event threshold,
or count a kinematically closed candidate as evidence of a qualified rate.
Product energy sharing, recoil and electron/fragment angles need an explicit
sourced or separately declared diagnostic model. The integrated cross section
alone does not specify them. No new closure is supplied by this inventory.

Keep the remaining H2 elastic, rotational/vibrational, other excitation,
attachment, ionization and tail coverage explicitly missing unless separately
qualified. Preserve missing e+OH/e+O and unknown recombination refusals.
Total H2 ionization cannot all become H2+; its dissociative/non-dissociative
branching must be resolved before using that total as an event hazard. An
alternative early O elastic component is feasible after the DCS units,
normalization, state population and low-energy coverage are resolved.

Necessary qualification for the next component: exact raw hash and target-state
binding; eV and a0^2-to-m^2 conversion using pinned constants; no endpoint hold
outside the table; separate physical zero/unknown intervals; monotone energy
grid and finite nonnegative rates; threshold/near-threshold and high-tail
refusal; CPU/GPU agreement; and event-level charge, nuclei, energy, momentum,
target debit and secondary inventory checks. Adding a supported channel must
not make the minimum scope guard report a complete network. Thermal support
can still reach unsupported intervals, so these finite tables do not justify
restarting the full physical run by themselves.

No qualified physical coupled EEDF was inferred. Empty or manufactured pilots
cannot bound reachability, and the older electron-only trajectory history
cannot establish ion/neutral channel closure. Number densities remain cm^-3
in simulation outputs. Cross sections here retain their source area units.

it does not export raw rate arrays, create a blob or alter any source table.

column labels. The OH PDF pages 9-10 were rendered and visually checked.
