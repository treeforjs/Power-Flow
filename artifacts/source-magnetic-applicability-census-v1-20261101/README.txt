Source magnetic applicability census

This artifact preserves an isolated diagnostic. It does not modify emission
rates, random marks, source guards, material laws or any production binding.
"Within the guard" means within the existing declared source approximation;
it does not establish a calibrated emission error or complete source physics.

The original job 654 fixture is replayed exactly: each of its two water settings
has 47 authored electron arrival samples, including all 21 refused samples,
6 source-held layers and 20 thermal surface handoffs. These are manufactured
phi = 2 eV, macro-weight = 1e-23 controls. No accepted owner is published.
The diagnostic records the individual marks, selected quadrature node, normal
and transverse energies, barrier overlap, distinct PIC handoff distance and
each magnetic term. Complete bounded source spectra and provenance are retained.

Collocation and clocks:
E and B are interpolated at each registered patch origin from the actual C19
accepted dc_seed at 0.749788055492 ns. The original fixture holds those arrays
over a 10 fs interval and labels the trial frame at its midpoint; it does not
solve an evolved midpoint field. Temperature is the accepted patch area mean,
which is uniformly 300 K in this fixture. It is not pointwise temperature at the
field interpolation point. Actual positions, axial slice centers, area, normal,
source/field/thermal stamps and identities are present in each spectrum record.

Observed guarded regime:
The extracting-face normal fields are about 5.503, 16.512, 27.536 and 39.565 kV/m.
Tangential B is about 0.668 to 0.761 T. Sample overlap distances are about 95 to
256 nm. The first refused sample has a 0.025786 eV tangential linear bound and
0.003330 eV quadratic term against a 0.025852 eV source scale: ratio 1.126 versus
the unchanged 0.2 cutoff. The normal-field zero-point term is negligible for
these nearly tangential fields. These terms describe the minimal-coupling
barrier approximation; the magnetic Lorentz force does not do work.

Separate nominal-reference probes:
With phi = 4.3 eV and the same fields/temperature/target macro-weight, the actual
arrival plan contains zero electron packets. The expected physical number per
patch per 10 fs is about 1.22e-61 to 1.64e-61. An explicitly separate set of
47 probes reuses the original manufactured quantiles in the new spectrum. Its
guard decisions are not an emitted-history distribution. In this thermal regime
changing phi shifts the sampled metal-normal energy and strongly suppresses the
current; it does not remove the magnetic applicability issue for those fixed
conditional quantiles.

Primary evidence:
Wilson (1966) (10.1063/1.1708797), verified through its publisher-deposited abstract,
supports 4.3 eV as an effective thermionic reference for well-outgassed
polycrystalline 304 in clean 1e-9 Torr vacuum. This does not calibrate an oxidized,
water-covered pulsed foil, its Richardson factor, or its microscopic enhancement.
D'Arcy and Surplice (1973) (10.1016/0039-6028(73)90115-5) reports surface-state-sensitive
work-function changes. No such response law is inferred or enabled here.
The magnetic Hamiltonian in Lebedynskyi et al. (2015), equation 28 supports the tangential
linear/quadratic structure. Its illustrative high-field case and the different
materials/geometries in Waites and Schwettman (1973) do not validate the numerical cutoff 0.2.
Full bibliographic metadata, access limits and primary-file hashes are in
PRIMARY-SOURCES.json. Raw papers remain outside the repository.

Remaining boundary:
No supported late populated source exposure was available for this census.
Vacuum Hall pre-run extrema are not spliced into artificial collocated samples.
A later pilot must census its actual exposed T/E/B/spectrum and retain every
refused mark. If an actual launch falls outside this model domain, it remains
refused; it needs qualified magnetized source physics rather than mark rejection
sampling or a larger cutoff. Production and material qualification remain false.
