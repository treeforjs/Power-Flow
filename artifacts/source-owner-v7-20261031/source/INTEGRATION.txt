Heat/source owner v7 -- what native v8, Hall and coordinator v8 must change to wire it in -- 2026-10-31

Nothing here has been integrated. Owner v7 at its benchmark settings is owner v6.1 (same digests and decisions;
the sealed v6 qualification passes against it), so coordinator v8 can switch the binding first and enable the
surface model per patch afterwards. Section numbers refer to API-v7.txt.

A. Step data flow with v7 (one accepted step n -> n+1)
  step n    Hall closed proposal produces first-hit sinks (hc::SinkRecord: native id, state, weight, charge, mass,
            incident proper velocity, native hit point, field-frame point, flight, boundary/axis, collected,
            conductor) and applies its own sink wall heat (incident kinetic energy) -> publication.
  step n+1  (1) coordinator maps the published collected sinks of step n on foils 0/1 to WallHit per slice owner;
            (2) wall = owner.respond(epoch, hits, run_seed, step_index) on every slice owner (pure);
            (3) quotes = owner.release_quotes(epoch, wall) -> arrival v1.1 plans n = SR(D/w0) neutral leaves (the
                stimulated release rides on the approved quantized route; arrival unchanged for neutrals);
            (4) emission: per patch and enhancement class k: owner.prepare_class(...) -> one arrival electron stream
                per (patch, class); births through owner.sample_birth (matching displacement, sub-cell return);
            (5) wall births (wall.births()) -> native births at the hit point at the step start;
            (6) owner.prepare(epoch, field, view, sources, handoffs, leaves, other, qualification, &wall,
                &interval_evidence) -> trial loads include the wall loads (channel surface-v7-wall-response);
            (7) Hall proposal with those loads; preflight; publication of every owner as today.
  The one-step lag of the wall response (hit in step n, response applied at the start of n+1) is <= dt; at 10 fs
  an emitted 5 eV electron moves 13 nm in that time (200 um cells). It is recorded per birth (parent hit time).

B. Native v8 (sink events feeding source births)
  B1 Expose the per-record first-hit sinks of the accepted step (native v6 FullTimeSinkRecord with the Hall-side
     fields) to the coordinator after publication, not only flows->sinks->tallies; keep them until the next
     step's source preparation (restart: part of the accepted state that the next step consumes).
  B2 New birth origin "surface-v7-wall-response": native accepts births authored by the source owner from a
     WallResponse: identity = (wall response content sha256, parent carrier id, ordinal), species, weight exactly
     the parent weight (exact count credit, one credit per birth: births = sum of event outputs, checked by the
     owner), position = parent hit point converted from the field frame (native = field - native_to_field_shift),
     proper velocity from the response, birth time = step start (lumped; the lag is recorded). The species catalog
     must contain every species the enabled channels can produce: with water chemistry v-current (e-, H2O, H2O+)
     the products are e- and H2O (H2O+ neutralizes to H2O; electrons from SEE/IIEE); fragments and atoms (H, OH, O,
     H2, from H3O+, OH+, O+, H+, H2+) appear only when those ions exist and need catalog entries first. Until then
     the coordinator refuses a wall response with a product outside the catalog (no silent drop).
  B3 Matching-plane emission births (sample_birth, API 5): a birth at face point + L n (L <= sub-cell extent,
     sub-micrometre to tens of micrometres) with a zero-duration bridge path from the face point to the birth point
     carrying charge -q w; native records the bridge with the birth (the field work F L q w is the ledger's
     resolved matching field work, no longer "unrepresented").
  B4 Born-and-collected emission births: an electron with outcome ReturnedSubcell returns within the sub-cell layer
     in the same step. Native must support a birth that is credited and sunk in the same step at the same point
     (or the coordinator does not stage it: then arrival v1.2 must accept "planned births withdrawn as sub-cell
     returns" with an exact count record). Net charge, electrons and energy of the cycle are zero (owner ledger).
  B5 Sink kinds: sinks on the short bridge / axial / outer planes stay absorbed (no wall model there) unless a
     patch with a v7 wall model is declared on those surfaces.

C. Hall (wall heat)
  C1 Keep the sink wall heat of collected carriers (incident kinetic energy at the impact point and time). The v7
     wall load of each event is the remainder (energy in minus energy out minus incident kinetic energy: work
     function of absorbed electrons, recombination energy of ions, adsorption energy of stuck molecules, minus the
     kinetic energy and work-function cost of every emitted or reflected particle). It arrives one step later as a
     source-trial SurfaceLoad, channel "surface-v7-wall-response" (hall_source_binding_v7::channel_of), with
     negative values allowed (energy leaving with secondaries/reflected particles). Hall must account the channel in
     its load ledger and accept negative authored heat on this channel (as for the desorption debits).
     Alternative (WallModel::hall_applies_incident_kinetic = false): Hall stops applying sink wall heat for
     carriers routed to v7 and v7 loads carry the whole wall heat; then the heat of the impact moves one step later.
  C2 Electron reference: an absorbed electron now deposits K + phi (phi in the v7 load). The coordinator/Hall
     energy ledger must move the collected electrons' work-function energy from "electron reference / unrepresented"
     into the wall heat it is now part of (no double count: the emission Nottingham load already debits E + Et =
     K/e + phi at birth).
  C3 Charged newborns from wall responses take their macro-charge from the conductor bank at the hit point (as
     emission births do); the incident sink already returned its charge to the bank in step n.
  C4 Bridge deposits for matching-plane births (B3): deposit the zero-duration bridge (charge-conserving path
     deposit, face point -> birth point) and book its field work; the absolute Gauss check must see the displaced
     charge as a path, not as a defect.
  C5 Step cap: the source owner's trial interval bound is OwnerOptions::interval.cap_s (configured; 1e-14 default).
     The remaining literal 1e-14 on the coupled path is Hall's neutral stream bound (kinetic_boundary.cpp:57,
     "neutral authoritative interval") -- a Hall version must make it configurable before particle-carrying steps
     can exceed 10 fs.

D. Coordinator v8
  D1 Binding: hall_source_binding_v7 (bind(hall, slice, OwnerOptions)); SourceSettings.surface per patch:
     surface_v7::ss304_surface(registry) for the model, default for the benchmark; record the registry
     (parameters-v7.json digest), the owner options and the wall seed in the configuration identity and receipts.
  D2 Sink -> WallHit: species = surface_v7::species_index(sink.state); patch = the slice owner of the hit z
     (clamp(floor((z - zmin)/dz))) and the face of the hit (boundary + axis + sign) and the along-coordinate of the
     patch partition; weight, incident proper velocity, field-frame point, hit time = begin + flight. Refuse a sink
     that maps to no patch with a wall model (unless the step is benchmark).
  D3 respond/quotes/prepare as in A; seed = the arrival run seed (the v7 key is salted, distinct streams), step =
     the accepted step index (< 2^40). One respond per slice owner per step, before release_quotes.
  D4 Emission streams: one arrival electron stream per (patch, enhancement class); stream ids patch*K + k < 2^16.
     sample_birth with BirthPolicy{subcell_extent = 0.1 h_min, return_extent = 0.1 h_min, source log-current
     timescale from the previous accepted state (dt / |d ln J|), image tolerances 0.03}; ReturnedSubcell -> B4.
  D5 Step length: choose dt so that t + dt is exact in binary64 (dt a multiple of ulp(t)); otherwise the sealed v3
     continuation check (end - t <= dt_s) can refuse an active source layer after one rounding (README Findings).
     Step controller: keep a RateSample history (owner.rate_sample(epoch, field, origins) per accepted step);
     criterion dt <= owner.interval_bound({prev, now}).bound_s when the owner has error targets; coupled maximum
     = min(Hall cap, source owner cap_s, Hall neutral bound C5). The owner refuses a step above its bound with a
     typed Refusal (step-size refusal: retry at the controller's smaller rung).
  D6 Ledger columns (per step): wall events by species; births by channel (elastic, rediffused, true, PE, KE,
     reflected, thermal, specular, fragment); stuck molecules; stimulated release (H_stim, D, n w0); wall energy
     quanta in/out/wall (exact int128 text); energy-capped, yield-clamped, outside-data and refused counts; returned
     sub-cell births; per-class emission counts and phase-map regimes of every patch (diagnostic).
  D7 GPU (optional): the wall kernel (device/sw7_kernels.cu) is bit-identical to the CPU reference
     (test_wall_gpu_v7); a GPU evaluator may replace the CPU loop of respond only behind a per-step bitwise check of
     its outputs against the owner's CPU evaluation of a keyed sample (or all events while counts are small).
  D8 Restart: the owner checkpoint format is unchanged (stock = coverage carries the surface history); the sinks
     of the last accepted step (B1) must be part of the restart state.

E. Acceptance tests for the integrated version
  E1 benchmark build: coordinator v8 with owner v7 at benchmark settings reproduces the v6.1/v7 ledgers bit for bit.
  E2 model build: every step's wall ledger closes exactly; Hall thermal closure includes the v7 channel; native
     birth credit = owner births; stock + released = initial + stuck exactly; GPU/CPU wall parity on the run's hits.
  E3 interval: adaptive steps above 10 fs with Hall and source caps raised, refused above the source bound.

F. With the SS304 phase thermal owner (ss304-phase-thermal-owner-v1-20261031)
  F1 Coordinator: adapt thermal.patch_reports() of the accepted phase-owner state to electrode_heat_v7::PhaseView
     (revision, time = the accepted clock, digest = the phase owner's state digest or a digest of the reports,
     PatchPhase{mean_T, max_T, liquid_fraction, melt_depth_m, recession_m, evaporated atoms Fe/Cr/Ni}) and pass it
     in OwnerOptions::phase; the patch layout of the phase owner (Config::patches) must equal the source owner's.
     Until the heat-v2 Coupler view is removed (owner v7.1: the Coupler remains the geometry/clock view), keep a
     heat-only slice Coupler with the same geometry for areas, normals and epochs; the temperatures come from the
     phase view.
  F2 Emission above 3000 K: OwnerOptions::emission_T_max_K = 5000 (the phase owner's domain); liquid patches emit
     through the liquid class (D4 streams: one more class per patch while a liquid fraction is reported).
  F3 Water: OwnerOptions::water_law_T_max_K = 3000; water_writeoff_T_K (e.g. 2900 K) so that no sub-w0 residue
     reaches the cap; the arrival owner keeps releasing D through SR(D/w0) below it.
  F4 Loads: source-owner loads to the phase owner (StepInput.loads): emission Nottingham, wall responses, desorption
     debits, water write-off; never evaporative cooling (phase-owner internal). Ledger columns: water desorbed
     (thermal, stimulated), water written off, metal vapor atoms (phase owner), side by side.
  F5 Metal neutrals (evap_mode 2): their first hits return to the phase owner as deposits (INTEGRATION 1.6 there);
     the source owner's wall kernel has no Fe/Cr/Ni species and must not receive them.
