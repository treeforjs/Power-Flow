Use gauge Az=Bn*y-Bt*(x-xs). Set xs=sqrt(c/F), Us=phi-2sqrt(cF), L=xs,
P=m*omega0*L, E*=m*omega0^2*L^2/e, hbar*=hbar/(P*L).
The sealed real symplectic matrix S maps canonical mode coordinates
(Qu,Qs,Qg,Pu,Ps,Pg) to (xi/L,y/L,z/L,px/P,py/P,pz/P).
