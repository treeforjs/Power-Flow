dependency-install side effects. The parent owns coupled integration, electron

No existing application source or production state is changed. Beta=1. The

Law and numerical domain
Let n be the actual material-outward unit normal and E the signed ONE-SIDED
vacuum total solved field on that actual patch at the declared stage. Define
F=-E.dot(n), because electron force is -eE. F>0 extracts electrons; F<0 retards
them. Electrode names and |E| do not choose the emission branch. Both electrodes
retain thermal supply, including backs and side faces with their actual normals.

All energies below are in eV relative to the metal Fermi level. With
c=e/(16*pi*epsilon0), U(x)=phi-F*x-c/x is the planar Schottky-Nordheim barrier.
Its saddle is at sqrt(c/F), lowered by2*sqrt(c*F). Below its maximum, numerical
turning points and a Gauss-Legendre integral give G=2sqrt(2me*e)/hbar integral
sqrt(U-epsilon)dx. A cosine coordinate removes endpoint singularities. D is
1/(1+exp(G)). Above the saddle, the signed action continues with the local
inverted-parabola slope. This is a stated above-barrier approximation, not an
exact quantum transmission solution. Curvature, band structure, oxide layers,
non-equilibrium electron temperatures and calibrated surface changes are absent.

S(epsilon)=kT*ln(1+exp(-epsilon/kT)); atT=0 it is max(-epsilon,0).
J=C integral D(epsilon)S(epsilon)d(epsilon), C=A0/(kB/e)^2 and
A0=4*pi*me*e*kB^2/h^3. There is ONE integral, never an RLD+FN sum. For F<=0 the
local material-to-vacuum step has D=theta(epsilon-phi): this is outgoing thermal
boundary supply, not the net current crossing a globally retarding potential.
PIC must transport and actually recollect returning electrons. This branch is
not a claim that a retarding field has a downhill SN saddle at infinity.

The current implementation's numerical domain is phi2..6eV, T=0 or1..3000K,
|F|<=5GV/m, positiveF>=1V/m, and barrier lowering<0.98phi. Unsupported inputs
raise errors; they are not clamped. These are numerical/model bounds, not SS304
phase limits. Actual T/phase data admission is a separate mandatory stage flag.
Current thermal data support773.15K is not silently extended by this law's3000K
mathematical tests. The very hot inherited heating history remains unsupported.
Near-zero positive F below1V/m is an explicit current numerical restriction;
the parent must not silently delete thermal supply when it encounters it.

Quadrature uses8-point energy cells,128 panels per resolved energy segment and
64-point action quadrature by default. Breaks resolve the Fermi edge, saddle and
decay widths. Tails extend48 thermal/field widths. Refinement is empirical
numerical qualification, not a proven global quantum/material error bound.
Log-sum-exp preserves tiny currents; true FP64 underflow is explicitly flagged
with finite logarithmic magnitude. No overflow/NaN is admitted. The source
ensemble is broad-band free-electron, not measured SS304 electronic structure.

Phase space from the same flux
Normal epsilon is sampled from the very quadrature weights defining J, i.e. a
documented quadrature-discrete spectrum. Given epsilon, transverse energy has
conditional density f_FD(epsilon+Et)/S(epsilon), Et>=0, and uniform azimuth. The
inverse CDF is used; the cold limit is uniform Et in[0,-epsilon]. The same
distribution yields the energy moment and each actual packet. Only the
zero-field Boltzmann limit reduces to a flux half-Maxwellian/cosine law with
mean outgoing kinetic energy2kT. FE packets never use the old thermal sampler.
Caller-owned draws, packet weights and event IDs avoid a second RNG/inventory.

Complete heat and reference ownership
For a sampled electron define H=epsilon+Et (Fermi-relative reservoir energy).
Complete Nottingham wall heat is -e*H per physical emitted electron, assuming
replacement at the Fermi level. The work function is already present in that
thermal energy cost; do not debit it again as wall heat. The emitted particle's
kinetic energy depends on the matching location and is not e*H.
At x in the image layer, K/e=H-phi+F*x+c/x. The explicit electron reference is
e*phi; image reference is -e*c/x; field/circuit work from the material to that
point is e*F*x. Therefore Qwall+K+reference+image=Wfield, exactly in this model.
Multiply each term by physical packet weight once. Mean heat is diagnostic:
never debit both mean heat and the sampled packet heat. The thermal adapter may
consume COMPLETE wall heat; K/reference/work are then decomposition diagnostics.

Usable strong-B matching
The simple sample_conditional branch calculates a matching layer from barrier
exit, image-force/energy tolerances and source variation, but REJECTS appreciable
gyroangle using actual local B. It is not used to bypass magnetic insulation.
The preferred strong-B API is begin_layer / advance_layer. It starts at a
calculated finite-energy overlap just outside the barrier, using the same
epsilon/Et. The overlap energy is a declared fraction of max(kT,Gamow decay
width), so its placement is a convergence parameter tied to an energy criterion,
not an invented spatial offset or measured quantum exit velocity. Initial
quantum projection is a stationary source approximation; quantum formation time
is not claimed to be solved.

Actual local E/B then evolve the particle in physical time with a relativistic
implicit midpoint/discrete-gradient step. Secant velocity
w=(u_new+u_old)/(gamma_new+gamma_old) is used for displacement and magnetic force;
the image gradient is c/(x_old*x_new). Thus magnetic work is zero and
deltaK+deltaUimage=q*E.dot(delta_r), subject to the recorded numerical guard.
Substeps bound gyroangle and relative normal displacement. Packets can remain
Active across accepted intervals, hand off to ordinary PIC, or return. No jump
across a resolved sheath/gap is permitted. The actual local constant-vector
field validity region (a linear-potential overlap), patch/mesh and physical
normal constrain the path, including lateral displacement.

Handoff requires small image force and energy, outward motion and enough normal
energy to restore the conservative remaining image tail. That tail decreases K
and increases image reference by equal amounts; it is NOT electrode heat or an
extra circuit energy source. Ordinary PIC then owns collective fields, with no
second analytic microscopic image force. On own return the explicit conditional
model is unit electronic accommodation at the emitter, not calibrated SS304
sticking, reflection, secondary emission or Pauli-blocked return scattering.
Collection restores electrode charge and the full reservoir/reference energy;
a static conservative emit-return cycle closes without a second heat debit.

The quantum SN supply itself is unmagnetized. A separate conditional guard
checks transverse canonical magnetic-energy shift and normal-field Landau scale
relative to the emission energy width. Its threshold is an applicability choice,
not a rigorous current error bar. Tests declare0.3 and record the ratio; default
is0.2. Refusal aborts the trial; no sample is dropped and resampled to obtain a
pass. A fully quantum magnetized metal barrier is not implemented.

Native-image overlap is a field-owner port
E must be the self-consistent coarse collective field, not a field to which an
analytic microscopic single-electron image force has already been added. If its
particle gather includes that electron's discrete conductor-image self response,
the owner must subtract it or bound the overlap below the chosen image-error
budget. On a fixed operator this response is explicitly calculable from the
single-physical-electron Green self energy
U_h=(e^2/2)*s(x)^T*(G_D-G_free)*s(x), with compatible derivative. The correction
would be U_SN-U_h; the library currently assumes self-excluded mean-field input
and rejects an already applied microscopic image force. Before creation, the
frozen field naturally excludes the trial electron. Active-packet continuation
must retain the same declared self-field convention. This is not permission to
remove collective space charge or impose shielding. The overlap subtraction or
quantified coarse-grid residual remains a parent field-admission obligation.

Atomic parent integration
At one accepted stage bind actual evolved T, solved one-sided E/B, area, normal,
mesh/material/hash and accepted-state revision. Prepare is pure. Use area in m2
(depth/height already included exactly once), and do not create a second emitter
or neutral/electron pool. Store Active LayerState in the ONE PIC inventory and
restart it. Only its subgrid owner advances it until handoff; normal PIC must not
advance it simultaneously or reapply its stored birth ledger.
Consume LayerTrial.exchange once. Deposit every CurrentPath with the parent's
compatible charge-conserving shape/edge path rule and boundary/circuit work
owner. Zero-duration quantum bridges are integrated current impulses over the
accepted interval: never divide by their zero path duration. All physical-time
flight paths and lateral motion are retained. After handoff ordinary PIC owns
the remaining accepted time; after collection the particle is removed once.
No stage may commit source heat/charge without its matching current, reservoir,
image/reference and field-work transactions. Roll back all on any refusal.
