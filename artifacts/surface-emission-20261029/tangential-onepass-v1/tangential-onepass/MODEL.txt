Conditional tangential one-pass thermal-field source prototype

Scope and selection

This component requires explicit selection of a continuum free-electron FD
reservoir and incoherent one-pass matching. It does not solve SS304's exact
magnetized band/edge spectrum, quantum coherence or arbitrary oblique fields.
The ratio hbar*e*B/(m*kT) is reported as a scale, not an error bound on those
omitted material/quantum effects. No foil model or native integration is
admitted. The original v1 library and nominal0.2 guard remain unchanged.

Domain:298.2..1500K; phi2..6eV; positive extraction F50..100MV/m; B<=40T
strictly tangent; E strictly normal. Numerical equality tolerances1e-12
do not provide a physical small-obliquity approximation. Both field vectors
and evolved temperature must belong to the same accepted Stage. The existing
material/planarity/equilibrium admission flags remain mandatory. Work function
4.3eV is a clean-surface benchmark with no actual foil/T calibration. Retarding
surfaces retain their separate thermal supply/return requirement; this source
does not cover them. It must not replace them by zero.

The actual local constant-field/geometry extent must contain a conservative
bound on the entire resolved quantum region. For vmax=sqrt(2e*Etmax/m),
when B*vmax<=F/2 the bound is2*(phi-Emin+delta)/(F-B*vmax); otherwise it is
(F+B*vmax)/(2*alpha), alpha=e*B^2/(2m). This follows from the descending
parabolic-barrier root or the subsequent local minimum, with the image term
lowering the potential. It is a domain check, not an arbitrary launch offset.
The later micro trajectories must also remain in the admitted region.

Joint source

Let n be the material-outward normal and d=n cross Bhat. The metal transverse
velocity is sqrt(2e*Et/m)*(cos(theta)*d+sin(theta)*Bhat). Minimal coupling
shifts its d component at distance x by -e*B*x/m. Therefore the effective
normal barrier, in eV relative to the Fermi level, is
 U=phi-(F+B*v_d)*x-c/x+alpha*x^2, c=e/(16*pi*epsilon0).
The cross term is retained. Its local saddle and subsequent allowed pocket
are found from the exact derivative. Below the saddle, the first real
turning-point action is numerically integrated, with a cosine endpoint
transform and Gauss-Legendre quadrature. Above it, a signed local parabolic
continuation is an explicit approximation. D=1/(1+exp(G)). This is one-pass
entry into a local allowed region; it is not terminal escape through an
infinite magnetic half-space.

With f(u)=1/(1+exp(u/kT)), energies in eV and C=A_ideal/(kB/e)^2,
 Jknown=C integral D(E,Et,theta)*f(E+Et) dE dEt dtheta/(2*pi),
 Qknown=-C integral (E+Et)*D*f dE dEt dtheta/(2*pi).
Gauss-Laguerre quadrature handles Et, periodic angular quadrature handles
theta, and segmented Gauss-Legendre integration handles E around each
canonical saddle. The same unnormalized weights determine J, complete
Nottingham heat and joint phase-space sampling. No half-Maxwellian sampler,
RD+FN sum, asperity factor, CL cap or shielding multiplier is inserted.

Known components and unresolved bounds

Unresolved channels are not assigned zero or used to renormalize the old MG
rate. Distribution returns Jknown, Qknown, nonzero upper bounds on unresolved
current and absolute heat, and a separate numerical-budget flag. Sampling
uses only this explicitly named component at Jknown; it refuses a large bound.
The upper bounds cover unresolved model integrals; resolved quadrature error
is assessed separately by refinement. They do not bound error against exact
quantum/material physics or certify a new native state without refinement.

The quadrature domain is Emin<=E<=phi+48kT and Et<=2phi+120kT by default.
Unknown local pockets/overlaps have a conservative raw FD supply bound. With
alpha=eB^2/(2m), k=B*sqrt(2e/m) and delta=eta*kT, all E>=Emin have a resolved
overlap for Et<=Etcrit if F-k*sqrt(Etcrit) exceeds both
3*(alpha^2*c)^(1/3) and2*sqrt(alpha*(phi-Emin+delta)). For a=Emin+Etcrit>0,
the remaining raw supply is bounded by C*(kT)^2*exp(-a/kT), with absolute
heat bound C*(kT)^2*(a+2kT)*exp(-a/kT). For a<=0, finite occupied-region
polynomial bounds plus the positive Boltzmann tail are used. Low-field/
strong-B states can have enormous unresolved bounds and remain unsupported.

For E<Emin at bounded Et, choose a fixed SN subinterval where the potential
is at least m>Emin. Its length L gives G>=a0*sqrt(m-E), a0=kappa*L,
kappa=2*sqrt(2me)/hbar. The omitted integral is bounded analytically by
2*exp(-a0*z)*(z/a0+1/a0^2), z=sqrt(m-Emin). Absolute energy follows the
corresponding third-moment exponential integral. For Et tails, split E at
-Et/2: FD bounds the upper half, and a triangular SN subinterval bounds the
lower half by G>=a*(phi-E), a=kappa/[8*sqrt(2)*(F/sqrt(Etmax)+k)]. Its
required inequality (phi+Etmax/2)^2>=16*c*(F+k*sqrt(Etmax)) is checked.
The high-E FD tail is bounded separately. Overlapping bounds are conservative.

Forward and inverse ports

Each sampled phase starts at a finite canonical overlap U=E-delta, or at the
local saddle for above-barrier phases. The transverse canonical momentum is
shifted by B; normal proper momentum is chosen to reproduce the exact declared
kinetic energy. The nonrelativistic barrier/reservoir model is distinct from
the relativistic classical kinematics used for transport.

Complete sampled heat is -e*(E+Et), electron reference +e*phi, image reference
-e*c/x and field work e*F*x. The quantum projection supplies its explicit
zero-duration q dx path. Its integrated current belongs to the accepted common
dt. The image/Lorentz layer uses v1's real-time energy-conserving transport.
The sealed v2 helper restores image reference on inward PIC re-entry. One
packet has one owner: Active layer or ordinary PIC, with provenance retained.

Returning quantum events use the SAME canonical D. The real reduced normal
Schrodinger problem has flux-normalized reciprocal transmission at fixed
canonical transverse mode. From the incoming momentum and overlap position,
the model reconstructs E and Et, then chooses absorption or reflection from
a caller-owned draw. Reflection atomically removes the provisional material
quantum bridge, cancels that bridge's charge/heat/reference/work exchange,
retains all actual micro paths, reverses normal momentum and keeps Active
ownership. It adds no new displacement, field work or heat. Absorption retains
the original complete reverse bridge exactly once. No second work-function
or mean-Nottingham debit is allowed. Unsupported return channels throw for
rollback; they are not silently absorbed or zeroed.

resolve_quantum_return must run BEFORE a provisional v1 recollection is
published. Parent must persist a distinct quantum-event counter/draw identity
alongside LayerState in its single native arena and restart journal. Native
rollback/restart for that new dispatch is not qualified by these component
tests. Using plain v1 advance_layer collection as the final new-model decision
would incorrectly impose unit absorption.

Validation scope

Current/heat quadrature refinement and B0 recovery, same-spectrum packet heat,
complete birth ledger, explicit oblique/non-normal-E refusal, and bounded real
outgoing-PIC/inward-image/material trajectories are checked. Canonical phase
is reconstructed every epoch. Partial-transmission fixtures must reflect and
later absorb; other packets may legitimately remain resident at4ps and are
not forced absorbed or deleted. These manufactured constant-field trajectories
are neither native Maxwell/thermal evolution nor a physical midpoint proof.
