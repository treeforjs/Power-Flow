2. Sinks (H3, native side): record format for the Hall side
 Motion admission accepts first-hit sink records in the binary flows record (section 4). One record per
 removed carrier, ascending native ID, disjoint from the endpoint rows; endpoints + sinks = census:
  FullTimeSinkRecord {
    u64 native_id
    kind     1 collected_at_conductor | 2 escaped_exterior
    surface  0 lower_foil | 1 upper_foil | 2 outer_xy | 3 short_bridge | 4 axial
             (= hall_driver::KineticSink; kind 1 for surfaces 0, 1, 3; kind 2 for 2, 4)
    u64 axis (0 x, 1 y, 2 z) and f64 plane_m: the hit plane (native coordinates)
    f64 fraction f in [0, 1] of the carrier's straight segment
    3 f64 segment_m D: full native displacement of the carrier flight F (F = dt, newborn: end - tau)
    3 f64 hit_point_m = start + f D componentwise, then [axis] = plane (kinetic_boundary.cpp:63 order)
    3 f64 incident_proper_velocity_m_s (neutral: unchanged u)
    f64 flight_s = F f (time to the hit from the carrier's start), f64 discarded_flight_s = F - flight_s }
  The carrier's path row: start = census position + shift, displacement = (hit_point + shift) - start,
  flight = flight_s. The current/charge deposits of the record cover the truncated path only.
 Native verification against native-finite-target-kinetic-policy-v1 (native port of the sealed
 first_kinetic_hit: same operands, operation order and tie rule; compared bitwise with the Hall helper
 on 2e5 segments in the tests):
  sink_record         kind/surface/axis inconsistent; nonfinite field; hit point != start + f D with
                      the plane coordinate; neutral: D != F u / gamma (Hall neutral-stream arithmetic) or
                      incident u != u; path flight/displacement not the truncated path
  sink_outside_step   f outside [0, 1]; flight_s != F f; flight_s > F; discarded != F - flight_s
  sink_not_crossing   native first hit of (start, D) is none, or a different surface/fraction/axis/plane
  sink_duplicate      a carrier removed twice, a sink of a carrier outside the census, a carrier with
                      both an endpoint and a sink, a census carrier with neither
  sink_partial_packet only some carriers of one logical packet removed (co-located carriers move together)
 Carriers without a sink record keep the v5 strict-interior endpoint rule and the v5 foil segment guard.
 Kinetic budget: K = sum over census in ascending ID of w (KE(end or incident u) - KE(start)) in long
 double, compared with the record's kinetic_change bitwise; work defect and guards as v2.
 Effect: removed packets leave the packet map (their IDs stay retired in the ever-ID set), their native
 rows leave the native rows; one journal row kind 7; cumulative per-surface totals updated.
 FullTimeSinkLedger (PreparedArena::candidate_full_time_flows()->sinks, also the sink row digest):
  accepted_step, transport_trial_sha256, carriers, records (as admitted), removed_packets (sorted),
  tallies per (surface, species state): kind, charge_e, carriers, exact weight, exact kinetic energy,
  surfaces (all five, ascending): kind, carriers, exact weight, exact kinetic energy, exact positive and
  negative elementary charge (weight x |charge_e| per species), records_root_sha256, ledger_sha256,
  row_sha256. Exact sums are ExactParticleCount values (units 2^-1074): weights are the carriers' binary64
  weights; kinetic energy terms are fl(w KE(incident u)) (one rounding each, pic_kinetic_energy_J).
 Hall side (charge banks and wall heat, separate Hall version): surfaces 0/1/3 (collected_at_conductor)
 -> conductor bank of that foil/bridge; 2/4 (escaped_exterior) -> exterior bank. Net free charge leaving
 the plasma = e (positive_charge_e - negative_charge_e) per surface; the incident kinetic energy is the
 wall heat input of collected carriers (exterior: energy leaving through the exterior); species internal
 and reference energies are given by the tallies' species and weights. The ledger digest binds the step.

8. Qualification boundary
 Births are credited from genuine arrival owner v1 plans and genuine source v5 leaves/candidates (sweep
 test). Motion with births and sinks comes from the test's zero-field reference provider (straight drift,
 sealed Hall first-hit helper; record sealed by an independent encoder), not from a Hall provider: the Hall
 version producing flows records (partial flights, first-hit truncation, truncated-path deposits,
 conductor/exterior banks, wall heat) is separate work. Plain v3 records come from the genuine Hall
 corrective v2 provider at zero total field. No field solve, no joint coupled step.
