Bounded aligned 3D collision atlas — design and CPU microcase

Status: geometric design and unchanged candidate-builder parity tested. This is
not a native-driver port, physical collision qualification, or a Debye/sheath
convergence result. No driver, solver, particle, source, GPU or production state
has been changed. The conditional 1 µm target requires independent refinement.

Geometry
Use the actual 5 mm foil width, .2 mm thickness, 5 mm gap and full 25 mm axial
height. In the prototype pilot frame, the box is [0,9]×[0,9]×[0,25] mm; lower foil
is [2,7]×[1.8,2] mm and upper is [2,7]×[7,7.2] mm through the full height. This
gives exactly 1.975e-6 m^3 of physical vacuum. Parent integration must derive these
from the accepted geometry owner, including its actual z origin, not copy this
nominal frame into an existing differently located state.

Split at all foil boundary planes first. The resulting 13 vacuum rectangles have
no solid cuts. Refine each rectangle by deterministic axis-aligned binary splits:
normal target h0 + .3*d to a material face, capped at 500 µm; broad-face tangential
maximum 125 µm and narrow side-face tangential maximum 25 µm; corner refinement
uses distance to the finite rectangle. No source is smeared, shifted or deposited
by this operation. These are collision bins, not finite elements; hanging bin
faces need no field interpolation or Poisson continuity operator.

Extrude each transverse rectangle through its own full-height z-axis family.
This avoids refining every transverse/axial direction whenever a normal layer
is refined. Every vacuum bin has V = dx*dy*dz, with no solid fraction and no
observation-slab depth. The atlas contains the whole kinetic collision domain;
extending the field mesh does not extend kinetic escape or collision bounds.

At 1 µm target there are 12,680 transverse leaves. The full Cell vector would
cost 10,144,000 bytes on the tested ABI (sizeof(Cell)=16), plus an estimated
~1.2 MB packed bounds/tree structure. Existing std::map cell indexing would add
allocator/tree/vector overhead if every empty bin were supplied; it must not be
rebuilt over the full atlas for each reaction. Measured test RSS below includes
that exhaustive comparison once, not a proposed production allocation strategy.

Independent local axial pair, identical transverse grid:
Away from a declared numerical refinement band use 1 mm z bins. Within 25 µm of
the foil rectangle use 500 then 250 µm z bins: 416,800 then 616,400 total bins.
Extending that numerical refinement band to 50 µm at the latter spacing gives
707,300 bins. No gas is excluded outside the band; it ONLY selects resolution.
The 125 µm local-z request exceeds the cap and remains refused. Global 250/125 µm
z, halved tangential bounds and .15 distance-growth requests also remain refused.
No automatic coarsening or partial grid is accepted. These cost controls provide
bounded sensitivity pairs, not convergence. Finer successful controls still need
an independently justified allocation/design; the cap is not silently enlarged.

Immutable atlas plus complete occupied-cell view
The native indexed candidate API already accepts arbitrary positive Cell volumes.
Keep the full geometric atlas immutable. At each accepted population generation,
locate EVERY positive native carrier with its original position and retain its
ID, state, weight, mass and proper velocity. Build the ascending-ID unique occupied
Cell view using the atlas's exact physical volume. Never normalize volumes to the
occupied total. Empty cells contribute no particle pairs and may be absent from
the supplied view. An occupied unknown cell, non-vacuum point, or incomplete
census refuses the whole request. No energy/support, packet, or source-status
filter may be used to make a cell appear empty. Products/population changes must
rebind the view to the new canonical prefix; a cached old view cannot be reused
without a complete verified transaction delta. Keep existing carrier, pair,
query, string, candidate and reaction caps independently of geometry-bin count.

Exact unchanged-builder evidence
The pinned pc::candidates header is copied unchanged and its source hash checked
against the owner before/after. A 128-cell variable-volume microgrid versus its
5 occupied cells gives 141 identical candidate rows. A second cost case uses ALL
46,726 preserved electron positions/weights/velocities for binning, plus one
clearly MANUFACTURED stationary weight-8 target per occupied cell and a constant
manufactured sigma. No actual gas distribution or physical rate is claimed.
The 634,000-bin atlas has 13,747 occupied cells in this actual-like case. Every
candidate field, query order, total rate and weight*rate sum is bitwise identical
between exhaustive and occupied views: 46,726 pairs/queries/rows. Removing an
occupied cell refuses before the first cross-section query. No Scheduler event
is selected or committed. One CPU-7 sample took .08447 s exhaustive versus .02369 s
occupied; the entire comparison process peaked at 108,600 KiB RSS. These are
mock-query costs, not projected coupled-run throughput. Geometry tests took
3.110 s and 54,144 KiB; census/atlas preparation 1.443 s and 44,112 KiB. All were
CPU 7, nice 15, one thread, shared lease, 4 GiB and 90 s bounds; one compiler job.

Source and common-stage ownership
Adsorbed matter is a surface reservoir and never acquires a fabricated 3D gas
volume. Exact material-face points remain refused unless the common stage gives
a valid explicit side/stage. The prototype can identify the adjacent vacuum bin
without moving that physical point; this is GEOMETRY membership, not permission
to react. A point 1 nm into vacuum is retained: no arbitrary vacuum strip is cut.

Active microscopic source-layer electrons are excluded from the ordinary mover
by their own operator owner, NOT automatically from all gas reactions. The current
microscopic layer has no gas-collision coupling. Its actual overlap, cross-section
support and optical-depth admission must be explicit in the common stage. The
atlas must expose membership/volume and preserve the owner tag, not invent a
zero collision rate, drop positive weight, double push it, or declare the missing
coupling qualified. This design does not resolve that operator-physics contract.

Coordinate, checkpoint and remaining controls
See API-CONTRACT.txt. Source, collision and field meshes can differ; all consume
the same canonical physical point. External heating/native-field coordinate
transforms occur once in the field adapter. No collision-side recentering,
rotation, rounding or observation-depth substitution is allowed. TOTAL E/B remain
owned by the Maxwell stage; the collision atlas adds no electrostatic field.

The CPU prototype uses exact integer-nm MANUFACTURED geometry and Fraction
positions to prove partitions. It never quantizes a native particle. The legacy
fixture's z-short_z translation is a declared read-only frame transform for this
test only. A native port must retain exact accepted binary boundary anchors,
typed frame identity and unmodified particle coordinates; native float boundary
parity and actual driver checkpoint integration have NOT been run.

Before pilot admission: preserve the existing uniform constructor/identity and
prove its source/bin/volume/query parity; native tests at faces, corners, outer
and axial endpoints; complete source/operator census with missing coupling
refused or separately admitted; exact charge/count/energy and original guards;
uniform-density rate integral and localized/disjoint overlap controls; independent
normal, tangential, axial and refinement-band sensitivities. Dense plasma may
need much finer response/field scales; 1 µm is a conditional starting bin size,
not Debye or sheath convergence. No actual PIC, trajectory or GPU qualification
is authorized by this design handoff.
