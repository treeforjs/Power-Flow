Magnetized-emission applicability and separate tangential one-pass prototype

The sealed source API v1 and the epoch/reverse adapter v2 are immutable.
Their nominal0.2 predicate is unchanged. The new guard-assessment library
analytically integrates that predicate's rejected transverse supply and its
Fermi-relative energy moment over the existing normal-spectrum quadrature.

For normal metal energy E and kT=t in eV, S(E)=t*log(1+exp(-E/t)). The v1
magnetic predicate is independent of azimuth and monotone in transverse
energy. At each normal node, its accepted transverse-energy cutoff is
Et*=m/(2e)*[(0.2*s - e*Bt^2*x^2/(2m) - hbar*abs(Bn)/(2m))/(Bt*x)]^2
when the numerator is positive; otherwise all transverse supply is rejected.
The conditional rejected probability is S(E+Et*)/S(E). The rejected total
Fermi-relative energy moment is
[(E+Et*)*S(E+Et*) + t^2*F1(-(E+Et*)/t)] / S(E),
where F1(y)=-Li2(-exp(y)). T=0 uses the exact uniform transverse distribution.
These quantities are weighted by nominal MG transmitted normal-node rates.
They are not predictions for the actual magnetized current. A numerical zero
on the retarding thermal-step branch is not proof of no magnetic correction.

All1056 legacy finite-offset probes were mapped at300/773.15/1490K. They
are not qualified native one-sided boundary fields. No probe is strictly
tangential-B, so a strict tangential replacement covers none of these proxy
states. Equal-probe current-weighted summaries are not surface-area integrals.
Unknown physical rates remain null, not zero or a renormalized accepted set.

Next prototype scope: explicitly selected continuum free-electron Fermi
reservoir plus locally stationary, one-pass canonical tangential-B barrier.
This retains the FD joint supply f(E+Et), rather than summing RD and FN or
reusing a half-Maxwellian. It does not solve the magnetized solid's detailed
band/edge spectrum or oblique-B scattering. The continuum supply and incoherent
one-pass matching must be explicit model assumptions. hbar*e*B/(m*kT) is a
diagnostic scale, not an asserted error bound for real SS304 electronic bands.

For B tangent to the material and E normal, minimal coupling gives the joint
effective normal barrier, in eV,
Ueff(x)=phi-(F+B*v_z)*x-c/x+e*B^2*x^2/(2m),
where v_z is the metal tangential velocity along n cross B and
c=e/(16*pi*epsilon0). The cross term is retained. D(E,Et,theta) is a
one-pass Kemble/JWKB first-barrier probability, with the same stated parabolic
continuation above its local saddle as v1. It is not a terminal escaping
current: the later Lorentz/image evolution can return particles. Closed or
unresolved local channels must retain an explicit unresolved contribution.

The finite-T joint integral is
J=C integral dE dEt dtheta/(2*pi) D(E,Et,theta)*f(E+Et),
Q_complete=-C integral (E+Et)*D*f dE dEt dtheta/(2*pi),
C=A_ideal/(kB/e)^2. Current, joint phase-space and complete Nottingham heat
must use the same weights. Canonical tangential momentum at overlap shifts
by -e*B*x. Image energy, work function reference, quantum-bridge q dx and
field work remain explicitly owned; B does no work. The reverse tail helper
v2 is reused without inventing an extra heat debit. Unit electronic return
accommodation is a separately selected conditional reservoir assumption.

Numerical truncation/admission strategy under derivation: retain known-channel
current and heat plus conservative nonzero upper bounds on unresolved/tail
contributions. A state with a large unresolved bound is not source-admitted.
For purely tangential B, alpha=e*B^2/(2m) and k=B*sqrt(2e/m). At a lower
normal cutoff Emin, local allowed pockets exist for all E>=Emin and
Et<=Etcrit if F-k*sqrt(Etcrit) exceeds both the saddle-existence threshold
3*(alpha^2*c)^(1/3) and2*sqrt(alpha*(phi-Emin+overlap)). Thus unresolved
rectangle phases have Et>=Etcrit. Their raw FD supply is bounded by the
positive triangular convolution starting at a=Emin+Etcrit, and must not be
replaced by zero. For a>0, current bound C*t^2*exp(-a/t), absolute heat bound
C*t^2*(a+2t)*exp(-a/t). Low-F states generally fail this bound.

For omitted deep E at bounded Et, a fixed subinterval on which the lower SN
potential exceeds m>Emin gives G>=a0*sqrt(m-E). The omitted current integral
is bounded by2*exp(-a0*z)*(z/a0+1/a0^2), z=sqrt(m-Emin), times C*Etmax.
Additional transverse and high-normal tails require explicit analytical bounds
and numerical refinement. These control the defined one-pass approximation;
they do not bound its error against the exact quantum/material problem.
