3. Is the update divergence-free in exact arithmetic?
 Update: B_f -= dt s_f, s_f = fl(sum_k c_k midE_k) (four plain binary64 products and sums; k_update_B and Model::prepare),
   B words updated with Words arithmetic; c_k = fl(+-1/h). Check: divB_c = sum_axes (B_a - B_b)/h (division).
 In exact arithmetic with the stored coefficients the discrete divergence of the update is not identically zero: for x and y
   edges the two cell faces give fl(1/dz)/dy - fl(1/dy)/dz = 1.43e-16 / (dy dz) (z edges cancel since dx = dy); with the check
   in coefficient form (multiplying by the stored fl(1/h)) it is identically zero. Measured in quad precision on the sampled
   steps: exact-arithmetic increment <= 1.3e-31 T/m per step (coefficient form <= 1e-34, quad noise) against actual increments
   1e-18 .. 1e-15 T/m. The drift is the rounding of the update (the plain double face sum s_f; the Words B update itself adds
   only O(eps^2)), plus the DC seed's rounding divergence (B0 from the DC solve in double).

 The refusal time is not a smooth function of h: the fine meshes drift more slowly in their worst cells (0.035 / 0.055 vs
   0.46 at similar times), but every mesh carries seed and drift divergence in cells whose |B| reverses (foil interiors under
   the skin-depth field reversal, cells near field nulls), so a refusal can come at any zero crossing; a 20 ns or coupled
   window cannot be guaranteed on any mesh with the current check. (The 25 um mesh is refused earlier, at construction:
   resolution-series F1.)

5. Fix options
 (a) Per-cell accumulated-admitted divB ledger (like the Gauss ledger): M_c += the step's computed divergence increment;
     check |divB_c - M_c| <= 128 eps scale_c.
     Needs (a1) a new per-step guard bounding the increment by its rounding size (e.g. |delta divB_c| <= 8 eps dt
     sum_f sum_k |c_k midE_k| / h_f), otherwise the ledger admits divergence created by a faulty update; and (a2) the seed
     divergence: M_c must start at the bootstrap value d_c(0) (admitted by the unchanged bootstrap check), because d_c(0) alone
     is refused at a zero crossing (the refusing cell: d_c(0) = -2.37e-14, bound at refusal 2.86e-14 and still falling).
     Risks: one changed and one new guard; a 12,150-cell (default mesh) state array in digests and checkpoints; the absolute
     check then only detects edits outside a step, and eps-level systematic divergence creation by the update stays admissible
     by construction. Physics bits unchanged (v3.1/v3.2 parity kept).
     A09 50 T/m: bootstrap refused (M = 0 before the bootstrap check; margin 3.5e11); injected mid-run refused (M unchanged by
     an out-of-step edit; margin 2.8e11 at 1.3 ns).
 (b) Structural: keep the discrete divergence at O(eps^2).
     (b1) Double-word face increments: s_f = sum_k c_k midE_k as a compensated exact-product sum (Words; fma two-products;
          error <= ~2 eps^2 relative), B_f -= dt s_f in Words; the DC seed B0 recomputed as the double-word curl of the seed
          vector potential (seed divergence O(eps^2)); the check unchanged (division form: exact-arithmetic mismatch
          <= 1e-31 per step, negligible) or in coefficient form (identically zero). Accumulated divergence after N steps
          <= N O(eps^2) sum|dB|/h (~1e-25 relative at 2e6 steps): a refusal would need a cell's |B| to fall below ~1e-12 of its
          history. Risks: physics bits change at the rounding level (B update and seed): bitwise parity with v3.1/v3.2 is lost
          and must be re-established against a new reference (host/device parity, component and coordinator parity at
          rounding tolerance, configuration identity change); the seed change touches the sealed Hall DC-seed construction
          (Hall-side recomputation of B0 from A0). Cost: a few extra fma per face (negligible).
          A09 50 T/m: guard unchanged -> refused, margins unchanged (3.5e11 bootstrap, 2.8e11 mid-run).
     (b2) Face fluxes Phi_f = B_f area_f updated by shared edge circulations (each edge term computed once, exact 4-term
          sums), divergence = sum +-Phi identically zero: same benefit, larger change (representation used by the rhs, energy,
          midpoint, push gather).
     (b3) Vector potential on edges, B = curl A on demand: divergence zero at every evaluation (no accumulation), but the
          rounding is relative to |A|, which grows with time (A = -int E dt): B precision degrades over long windows.
 (c) Budget floor: R_c += 8 eps sum_f |dB_f| / h_f per step (state), check |divB_c| <= 128 eps scale_c + R_c(+ seed term):
     self-contained, physics unchanged, but the allowance grows with the field's total variation (a loosening); A09 margin
     > 1e11 to 20 ns.

6. Recommendation
 (b1) for Hall v3.3: it removes the cause (O(eps) rounding of every B update and of the seed) instead of admitting it, adds
 no state and no guard, keeps the absolute divB check's meaning and its A09 margins, and costs nothing per step. v3.3
 already changes several device paths and needs a new parity baseline, so the bit-level change of B can be qualified there
 (host and device in the same Words operations; parity against v3.2 at rounding level; the 2.75 ns 10/100 fs cross-check and
 the 20 ns pre-run repeated). If bit parity with v3.1/v3.2 must be kept, (a) with (a1) and (a2).
