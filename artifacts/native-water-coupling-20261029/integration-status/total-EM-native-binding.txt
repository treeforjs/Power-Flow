Native total-field transport and axial field/heat binding, version 1

Entry points
  native_total_field_transport.hpp: foil::prepare_native_total_field_transport
    is the 2D3V operator binding.
  axial_particles.hpp: axial::prepare_native_total_field_transport is the 3D3V
    overload used by the axial feed/short pilot.
  axial_fields.hpp: axial::Fields::prepare returns a candidate total field and
    field/circuit/conduction/outer/short-current receipts without committing.
  axial_surface_path_port.hpp: import_surface_paths (relative coordinates) and
    import_global_surface_paths (source-v2 absolute coordinates) consume already
    weighted micro-layer paths, including zero-duration quantum bridges.
  axial_thermal.hpp: Thermal::prepare/preflight/commit binds copied sealed native
    electrode_heat::Coupler owners, one per foil per axial slice.
  axial_system.hpp: prepare_joint/commit_joint is the bounded Joule-only reference
    coordinator. It deliberately rejects particle collection until a boundary
    owner is supplied. Root's common driver owns source/event/collection routing.

Sole owners and immutable stages
  Maxwell owns TOTAL E and B. Call no GapPic::solve_fields, electric_field,
  set_external_fields, or advance in this EM mode. The old electrostatic-plus-
  external mode is a different mode; the total-field API rejects its enum.
  Only pic_boris is reused for momentum. The work displacement, path gather,
  deposition, and joint nonlinear iteration have their own checks; a Boris call
  alone does not establish energy closure.

  Pass an immutable charged GapParticle view from the ONE canonical arena. Neutrals remain with their streaming/reaction owner; a temporary charged view is not a second accepted inventory. The adapter
  owns no persistent particle population and allocates no IDs. Its updates and
  boundary endpoints are staged proposals with the same IDs, q, m, and weight.
  The caller alone applies removal/replacement/creation, ownership handoff, and
  ID allocation in the global accepted transaction.

  NativeInterval requires NativePhase::full_time_work_conjugate_v1 and binds accepted_revision, nonlinear trial_stage, begin_s, end_s,
  authoritative dt_s, and arena/field/mesh/material/species identity strings.
  end_s must equal begin_s + dt_s. Never reconstruct dt by subtracting rounded
  absolute clocks. In each nonlinear iteration, re-evaluate transport from the
  same accepted arena against that iteration's midpoint total fields.
  Only a converged jointly admitted trial advances field, arena, and heat clocks.

Motion and source paths
  Canonical active micro-layer electrons appear in the arena and in
  layer_owned_ids. They count in the all-species plasma guard but ordinary
  transport excludes their motion. Exclusion IDs absent from the arena reject.
  The source must seal and prepare its path/thermal/image/reference transactions
  against the same accepted revision and trial field stage.

  Current vectors are edge loads in A m, the integral of physical J times the
  compatible vector basis. They are NOT raw particle current moments and NOT
  nodal J samples. Deposit q*D/path_shape over the COMMON accepted dt, including
  zero-duration tunneling bridges. Do not divide by a path's flight time. Charges
  in micro-layer CurrentPaths already include weight; do not multiply by weight
  or the represented depth again. Relative path vectors retain global Cartesian
  components: add the physical patch origin, do not rotate B.

  Native transport returns initial/endpoint nodal charge in C, integrated field
  work in J, kinetic change, and continuity/work defects. A collected endpoint
  remains in the current/charge receipt even though it is absent from updates.
  The common driver must route endpoint charge and kinetic energy exactly once
  to electrode/exterior/short owners before committing the arena removal.
  Source image/internal/reference/Nottingham partitions are separate owners;
  field_work_J is booked once against the field/circuit ledger.

C19 current drive and geometry
  The impressed feed branch carries the supplied TOTAL current, upper to lower
  at z=+12.5 mm, with distributed cross-section contact weights. A PEC short sheet
  connects the foils at z=-12.5 mm. Interior foil fields remain resistive/free.
  Terminal voltage is predicted as minus E's line integral along the unit feed
  branch; positive voltage means lower terminal minus upper terminal. No
  conditional gap-voltage pulse is imposed as a second constraint.
  Region measurements use the exact incidence operator and include conduction,
  plasma, displacement, transverse transfer, and outer-boundary terms. There is
  no independent +/-I condition on the solid when plasma conducts.

  All scalar-potential derivatives in x,y,z are retained. Initialization solves
  a declared supported-start DC scenario with phi=0 at the short, then extends
  phi through vacuum and computes a closed-loop magnetostatic B. The magnetic
  gauge uses tangential A=0 at the outer box and interior Coulomb test functions.
  This is a recomputed finite-loop scenario, not replay of a sparse native Az/rate
  pair. The original in-plane B orientation is preserved in the foil core; end
  fields are allowed by the actual 3D geometry. No Hall velocity is added.

Thermal and work
  The frozen native SS304 evaluated-solid model is explicit: 300..1500 K,
  rho_ref=8000 kg/m3 scenario, fixed reference volume, Cp-as-Cv approximation.
  This is not a measured foil-density calibration or a compressible EOS.
  Conductivity is sampled from accepted thermal state for each next EM interval.
  The same midpoint solid current that debits field energy supplies cell Joule
  power. Its edge and cell quadratures agree. Each thermal slice uses height=dz
  once; no second extrusion or added Joule source is permitted.
  Thermal prepares all owners before commit. A rejection retains all accepted
  states. Thermal numerical energy residual is reported separately with the
  native model's unchanged roundoff bound; it is not injected as heat.
  The pilot omits axial heat diffusion, short heating, mechanics and vapor. The
  parent mechanics temperature limit and source-data limits remain applicable.

Scope
  The small uniform air box and coarse axial grid qualify operators and bounded
  coupled steps only. Production must use converged wide exterior and axial
  resolution; the independently tested graded exterior must not revert to 9 mm.
  Root owns the full arena, emission/reaction scheduler, broader boundary/heat
  transactions, production coordinator, GPU integration, and long-run launch.

Read phase-and-source-integration.txt before connecting the actual SSA/source-v2
operators. It gives the variable-dt full-time schedule, typed phase rejection,
source ownership/handoff rules, and the current accepted-start versus midpoint
source-field integration gate. C++20 is required by these headers.
