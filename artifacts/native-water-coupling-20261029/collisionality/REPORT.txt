Resolution and plume dilution
On the actual 25 µm field grid, median rho = 1.913 µm; omega_c*dt spans .00687–.04010
for dt = 4.7763 fs. Subcell orbits can be pushed in smooth fields: dx > rho alone is
not an invalidity proof. Temporal gyro resolution does not validate gradients,
source placement, boundaries or collision volumes. Prior native magnetic cells
near broad faces are 104.17 µm in the gap and 466.25 µm at the back.

The old electron-only peak is 5.44253e5 cm^-3. Local nonrelativistic central-velocity
second moments give positive Maxwellian Debye PROXIES in 1395/1565 occupied cells,
minimum 19.29 mm; 170 zero-variance cells have no usable temperature/Debye estimate.
Peak classical omega_pe*dt = 1.988e-7. Nonneutral, anisotropic, finitely sampled
particles are not a Maxwellian equilibrium; these are not actual shielding lengths.

For the named fully released .33-monolayer column uniformly filling 25 µm,
n_H2O = 1.716e17 cm^-3. Hypothetical ionized fractions 1e-6, 1e-3, 1 at Maxwellian
1 eV give lambda_D = 17.95 µm, .5675 µm, .01795 µm and omega_pe*dt = .0001116,
.003530, .1116. The fully ionized nominal Debye sphere contains only ~4.15 electrons,
so weak-coupling/continuum assumptions themselves need scrutiny. No shielding or
quasineutrality is imposed. These scenarios warn about future mesh, timestep,
particle statistics and closure requirements; they do not predict a water plasma.

Smearing a 1 µm layer over a 25 µm reaction cell reduces peak neutral density 25-fold;
for .1 µm the factor is 250. Normal constant-energy column optical depth and inventory
can survive while reaction overlap does not. True rate integrates n_e*n_neutral*k(E)
over physical 3D volume. Coarse products can dilute a shared near-wall peak OR create
spurious overlap with electrons outside the plume. The full-column mean known-
support total-scattering nu*dt is .00607 for 1 µm and .06066 for .1 µm; these are
spectrum averages with gaps, not accuracy guarantees or maxima. Observation slab
thickness must never replace physical collision volume. This identifies needed
support/overlap/refinement evidence, not one cause of the halo or forced screening.
