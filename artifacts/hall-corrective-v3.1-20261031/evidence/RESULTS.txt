6. Absolute Gauss allowance (report only; no guard changed)
   Observation (a4/gpu-gauss-trace-10fs, -100fs; diagnostic runs): the vacuum step is refused by the absolute Gauss
     constraint at step 48,650, t = 1.2363 ns, node 3747 (short plane at the upper foil inner face): defect 1.733447e-23 C
     against bound 1.733430e-23 C. With dt = 100 fs (diagnostic configuration, dt upper bound raised for that run only)
     the refusal comes at step 4,833, t = 1.2330 ns: the drift is per unit simulated time, independent of dt. The pilot
     (identical field evolution) would have been refused at the same time. The pilot's max/max ratio (0.074 at 81 ps)
     understates the per-node ratio, which is the guard criterion: 0.12 at step 8,000, 0.49 at 0.99 ns, 0.997 at 1.235 ns.
   (1) Tracking: the defect does not track ||b||: the rhs norm rose to 1.75e-6 at 0.87 ns and fell to 3e-7 at 1.03 ns while
     the worst-node defect grew linearly throughout (3.56e-28 C per 10 fs step, 3.6e-14 C/s), and the terminal voltage rose
     to 50 kV and fell to 23 kV. The bound of the solves' residual charge, sum_k sum_{e at n} sqrt(M_e)|rho_e|/len_e, accounts
     for at most 4.0e-24 C (23 %) of the maximum drift and for nothing at the refusing nodes. The drift equals the
     accumulated per-node Gauss increments: drift_n / sum_k |Q1-Q0-(div plasma+div metal+div outer)dt|_n = 0.997-1.000 at
     the worst nodes (values above 1 only in the first step, at defects below 6e-27 C). Mechanism: in exact arithmetic
     Q1 - Q0 - expected = -div(ampere(mid B)) dt + div(r) dt with div(curl^T x) = 0; in floating point the rounding of the
     quasi-static ampere and metal/short currents (~6 kA through the short plane) is nearly the same every step, so it
     accumulates linearly, scaling with the currents and with dt per step. Each step's mismatch is admitted by the existing
     increment guard (<= 1e-22 + 2e-10 activity), but nothing carries it into the absolute allowance, which only covers
     the seed solve (eps0 x 2e-13 ||rhs_DC|| = 1.73e-23 C) plus 256 eps x local magnitudes (1e-27 C at node 3747).
   (2) Extrapolation (worst node, drift rate scaled with the C19 current): 1.2e-21 C at 10 ns, 5.6e-21 C at 20 ns,
     9.6e-21 C at 25 ns (constant rate: 3.3e-22 / 6.9e-22 / 8.6e-22 C); longer adaptive steps do not change it.
   (3) Principled allowance (a correction, not a loosening): per node n keep in the charge ledger (like residue_C, part of
     the ledger record and the checkpoint) A_n += |delta_n(k)| + |c_n(k)| for every accepted step k, where delta_n is the
     step's Gauss increment mismatch that its own increment guard measured and admitted and c_n the endpoint continuity
     defect admitted by the continuity guard (coupled steps), plus the eps^2-level rounding of the two ledger word
     additions; the absolute check becomes |defect_n| <= seed bound_n + A_n. By the triangle inequality on
     defect_n(k) = defect_n(0) + sum of the admitted per-step changes, the absolute check then refuses exactly the charge
     inconsistencies that did not arise inside admitted steps (bootstrap, restore, injected charge), which is its
     purpose; it admits nothing a step guard refused. Measured with this allowance the per-node ratio stays <= 0.47 to
     1.24 ns (it tends to 1 from below while the drift dominates; a factor 2 on A_n keeps it near 0.5). An a priori
     alternative (no measured quantity), analogous to the endpoint tolerance: A_n += gamma eps dt sum_{e at n}
     (sum_b |C_eb H_b midB_b| + |metal_e| + |exterior_e| + |displacement_e| + |plasma_e|)/len_e with gamma from the
     operation counts. A09: 4.81e-11 C inconsistent charge vs A_n <= ~1e-20 C at 25 ns: refused with a margin of ~5e9;
     divB is not affected (1.108e-12 T/m, ratio 0.0035, constant to 1.24 ns; 50 T/m vs bound ~3e-10 T/m: margin ~1.6e11).

7. Step-size assessment for the particle-free pre-run (report only; default bound stays 1e-14 s)
   Drive: C19 is piecewise linear with knots 0.5-2.5 ns apart; |dI/dt| 1.6e12-9e12 A/s; relative current change per
     10 fs step 0.6-3.7e-6. The implicit midpoint step integrates a linear drive exactly between knots; only the kinks
     matter, so dt up to ~1/100 of the knot spacing (5-25 ps) resolves the waveform.
   Conductor: model sigma(300 K) = 1.391e6 S/m; skin depth sqrt(t/(mu0 sigma)) = 24 um at 1 ns, 107 um at 20 ns; the
     foil (200 um) is one 200 um cell thick, so the slowest resolved magnetic diffusion time mu0 sigma h^2 = 70 ns and
     its fastest mode ~7 ns: both far above 1 ps.
   Vacuum EM: transverse light crossing 30 ps, axial 125 ps; Courant number c dt/dx 0.015 at 10 fs, 1.5 at 1 ps; the
     implicit scheme is unconditionally stable; phase error per period (omega dt)^2/12 = 3e-4 for a 100 ps period at 1 ps.
   Thermal: Joule power 1.3 MW at 6 kA (two foils, 0.036 ohm); estimated foil heating 15-25 K by 20-25 ns; sigma(T) lags
     one step (relative change per step ~1e-9 at 10 fs).
