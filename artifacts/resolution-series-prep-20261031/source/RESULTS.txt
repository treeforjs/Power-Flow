0. Findings in short
  F1 Hall v3.1 admits the 200, 100 and 50 um meshes but refuses the 25 um mesh (360x4+1) at construction: the DC seed's
     Coulomb-gauge vector-potential solve fails its independent residual check ("DC Coulomb vector: field solve independent
     residual failed"). This is not an iteration limit: the evaluation error of the double-precision residual itself is 2.3e-13
     of ||b|| at 25 um, above the 2e-13 target; six further correction cycles stall at 3.07e-13. No preconditioner can pass this
     check; the seed needs a Hall-side change (section 3.2). 360x8+2 is refused the same way.
  F2 The resident field step costs 2.8 / 8.6 / 32 ms per 10 fs step at 200 / 100 / 50 um (about 4x per halving, linear in edges),
     ~119 ms at 25 um (112 ms measured on the 2.4M-edge proxy 180x16+4 with the same metal cells). The host independent residual (sealed x87 sequential norm) is 0.5 / 2.3 / 10.5 ms of it and
     grows with the edges; it and the host total-field digests are the parts that do not move to the device in v3.1.
  F3 Jacobi PCG iterations grow with the Courant number c dt/h (about 29 C for C >= 1): at dt = 10 fs only 4-5 iterations on every
     mesh, but the resident maxit of 200 caps particle-free steps at about 1 ps on 50 um and about 0.55 ps on 25 um. The GMG
     prototype keeps 20 iterations at 1 ps and 29-31 at 2 ps independently of h (100/50/25 um), meets the independent residual
     criterion, is bitwise reproducible and CPU/GPU bit-identical (section 4).
  F4 Device memory of the resident field is 887-901 bytes per edge (allocation model = measured peak, exactly): 2.31 GB at 25 um,
     not the ~200 B/edge assumed in PLAN.md section 2 (which gave ~60M edges on 12 GB; the real figure is ~11M edges on 12 GB and
     ~25M on 24 GB). Isotropic refinement in z is out of reach (50 um: 47.5 GB; 25 um: 379 GB).
  F5 The absolute Gauss allowance drift (Hall v3.1 RESULTS section 6) grows faster on finer meshes: worst per-node ratio rate
     1.5e9, 3.8e9, 6.1e9 per second at 200/100/50 um in the first 4-10 ps (linear projection to refusal 1.40, 1.01, 0.91 ns;
     the 200 um run of record refused at 1.236 ns). No pre-run or coupled window past ~1 ns is possible on any series mesh before
     Hall v3.2's accumulated allowance.
  F6 Time step: at the 10-25 ns window currents (50-150 kA) the gyro criterion (c_gyro 0.025, |B| bound 1.75-1.96e-4 T per A)
     limits particle steps to 14.5 / 7.3 / 4.8 fs at 50 / 100 / 150 kA on every mesh, and the displacement criterion for
     relativistic electrons to 8.3 fs at 25 um. Debye resolution at 3 eV holds up to 4.1e15 / 1.7e16 / 6.6e16 / 2.7e17 m^-3
     on 200 / 100 / 50 / 25 um.
  F7 Macroparticle control: the merge/split component conserves count and charge exactly and books momentum/energy residuals
     exactly; byte-identical over 1/2/5/8 partitions; CPU/GPU bit-identical on 2e6 carriers (merge-split/RESULTS.txt).
  F8 Per-carrier host work dominates every series member with >= 1e5 carriers per species with today's code: the closed Hall
     step alone costs 5.6-6.0 us per carrier on every mesh, the coordinator ~15 us more (section 7). The mesh-dependent coupled
     floor is 10 / 35 / 142 ms on 200 / 100 / 50 um and 636 ms on the 2.4M-edge proxy of 25 um (3 fixed-point iterates, each
     with a host independent residual and host total-field digests).
  F9 Box size (circuit finding: the 9 mm box underestimates the port inductance): graded meshes with the stated cell size over
     the 9 mm core (foils, gap, kinetic box) and cells growing by 1.1 per cell to 35 or 45 mm reproduce the uniform-box DC
     port inductance to 0.03 % (200 um: 16.044 nH at 35 mm, 16.148 nH at 45 mm; uniform 16.047 / 16.151) at 6-15x fewer edges;
     45 mm is within 0.7 % of a 90 mm box (16.264 nH) and the mesh dependence at 45 mm is +1.0 % (100 um, 16.311 nH) and +0.26 %
     more (50 um, 16.353 nH). Graded 25 um to 45 mm: 4.03M edges, 3.57 GB resident (fits 12 GB). Hall v3.1 runs only uniform
     meshes: it admits the large uniform boxes at 200 um (n = 175/225) and 100 um (n <= 500) but not 50/25 um (n > 500, and
     ~15 GB / ~60 GB); graded boxes need a Hall version with per-index transverse coordinates and a kinetic box decoupled from the
     field box (section 1b).

4. Deterministic GMG preconditioner (precond/; OUT/b5, OUT/b6 gmg-*; GPU: my PCG kernels with host scalar read-back per iteration)
  Design: CG on Hall's scaled operator A = M^-1/2 K M^-1/2, K = M + dt/2 (S+Y) + dt^2/4 C^T H C, with the Hall gpu1 stopping rule;
  preconditioner = one symmetric V-cycle on K: x-y semi-coarsening (factor 2, or 3 for odd counts: 360-180-90-45-15-5), axial cells
  kept; lowest-order edge-element prolongation commuting with the gradient; Galerkin coarse operators stored as three parts (mass,
  conduction+admittance, curl-curl) so a dt change is a linear combination; Hiptmair hybrid smoother (l1-Jacobi on K, then l1-Jacobi
  on G^T K G mapped back with G; G with phi = 0 on the PEC short nodes), pre (edge, node), post (node, edge); coarsest level 4+4
  sweeps. Every product is a CSR row loop in ascending column order compiled identically for the host and NVRTC (gmg_core.h);
  dot products use the fixed TPB 256 x NBLK 512 tree that the CPU reference reproduces; no atomics. Acceptance: the Hall v3.1
  HostApply (bitwise the sealed apply) and foil::norm against max(1e-30, 2e-13 ||b||).

10. Open issues and what will not scale (details in the sections above)
  - Box: the 9 mm box underestimates the port inductance by ~32 % (F9); graded 35-45 mm boxes are ready as definitions but need
    a Hall version with per-index coordinates and a kinetic box separate from the field box.
  - 25 um not admitted by Hall v3.1 (F1): needs one of 3.2 (a)-(c) in a Hall version.
  - Absolute Gauss allowance drift reaches refusal near 1 ns on every series mesh, sooner when finer (F5): Hall v3.2 fix required
    before any pre-run to T_on or coupled window past ~1 ns.
  - Host O(edges) work per step and per fixed-point iterate: independent residual (x87 sequential norm), total-field digests of the
    midpoint (four per coupled step in coordinator v6.1), source-field copies; at 25 um these are tens of ms each. A device-side
    compensated residual and block digests (as for the state) are needed for 25 um.
  - Per-carrier host work (Hall v3.1 ~5 us/carrier, coordinator total ~20 us/carrier): 1e6 carriers per species is out of reach
    until native v8 (exact sums, Merkle motion records) and device-side deposits/digests land (section 7).
  - Jacobi maxit 200 caps particle-free steps at ~0.55 ps on 25 um; the GMG prototype removes this (not integrated).
  - Memory per edge is 4.5x the plan figure (F4); axial refinement at 25 um beyond 16 axial cells does not fit 12 GB.
  - The pre-run from 0.75 ns to T_on is per mesh (no transfer of a Hall state between meshes); the DC seed solve is per process.
  - Cross-architecture (sm_120) bit identity of the resident step, the GMG kernels and the merge/split kernels is not shown.
