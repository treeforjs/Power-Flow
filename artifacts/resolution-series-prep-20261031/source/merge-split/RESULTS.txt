8. Open issues and limits
 - Relocation work: booked but not small in long cells with field gradients (~3e-5 of the transformed kinetic
   energy in the test field at the 45 mesh). Sub-bins bound it at the price of fewer merges; the policy
   choice (sub[3], cadence, targets) for the pilot is open.
 - Merging degrades the in-bin distribution (two outputs per group): the bin resolution and k_max are
   policy; no measurement of EEDF-tail distortion or of the noise reduction was made here.
 - Not integrated: native v8 and Hall v4 do not exist yet (INTEGRATION.txt lists their work). The relocation
   deposit is a port of the Hall arithmetic, validated here against the Hall continuity formula only; no
   coupled step with a field solve has been run. The DepositSupport extension (R <= 3 for relocation rows)
   is specified, not implemented.
 - GPU relocation-current deposit not implemented (CPU reference only); the Hall GPU push deposit is meant
   to take the relocation records (INTEGRATION.txt 3.8).
 - Host wall time on the GPU path is dominated by host-side result unpacking, encoding and hashing
   (66-267 ms per 1e6) rather than device work (8-14 ms per 1e6); a resident integration would keep outputs on
   the device and hash on the device (as Hall v3.1 does for its state).
 - The per-group kinematics thread holds sequential double-word sums and one Philox stream; segments are
   processed one thread each, so a cell holding a very large fraction of all carriers serializes formation
   (not seen in these sets: device formation 2-9 ms).
 - Cross-architecture bit identity (sm_89, sm_120) not shown; only sm_86 was available.
