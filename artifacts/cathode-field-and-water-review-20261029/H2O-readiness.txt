Required implementation and data
1. Qualify the common source/field history. Use temperature and native magnetic/inductive fields from the same heating solve and an explicit pulsed voltage model. The new matched256x32 quick heating is available, but hot material phases are still absent. The old9mm electric exterior is materially sensitive: fixed-state9->25mm changes weighted vector E by14.03% in the finite gap and39.90% over all saved particles. Source birth locations passed the audit, but sheath/field-domain/full3D convergence remain unresolved. Changing histories requires a fresh supported-start PIC run.
2. Bind the neutral problem. Separate residual gas from finite adsorbed water, calibrate pressure/composition/gas temperature where available, and specify monolayer definition, coverage, patch masks, release kinetics, emitted velocity and internal states. Both complete foil perimeters at25mm height total5.20e-4m2; actual active masks control inventory. Run declared manufactured H2O scenarios while calibration is incomplete; do not present those as measured gas conditions.
3. Freeze physical collision data. Include elastic/momentum transfer, rotation/vibration/excitation, neutral dissociation, parent and dissociative ionization, attachment, and relevant product/heavy reactions. Each needs units, thresholds, incident-energy support, uncertainty/provenance, exclusive products, secondary-electron energy/angles, fragment recoil and internal/radiative energies. Inclusive partial-ion yields cannot be sampled independently as exclusive parent-removal events. Total ionization must not be assigned solely to H2O+. Unknown high-energy support must stop/flag a model, not silently hold the last table value or discard the tail.
4. Implement conservative online collision transactions. Debit one neutral parent, update/scatter the primary, create all secondaries/ions/anions/neutrals with matched weights and states, and deposit their charges/currents in the same accepted interval. Preserve H/O nuclei, charge, momentum and total event energy; record parent/channel/event lineage, rollback/replay and restart provenance. Use one electron number/energy owner: PIC/MCC electrons plus Raikou heavy/state kinetics is a viable architecture; evolving the same electron inventory independently in both would double count.
5. Couple feedback. Electron cooling, neutral depletion, ion/anion trajectories and surface collection must change subsequent fields and reaction probabilities. Hall/current magnetic feedback requires the compatible EM adapter and resolved intended drift direction. Postprocessing electron-only frames is useful screening, not a fully coupled fragmentation history. Complete external-field/circuit/boundary work accounting remains a separate requirement for global energy claims.
6. Validate in stages. Forced-event stoichiometry/energy/restart tests first, then monoenergetic-water collision swarms, weighted non-Maxwellian EEDF integration and spatial neutral overlap, ion-feedback controls, and mesh/time/macroparticle convergence. Use the published reduced source as a declared comparison closure with a physical conversion length and documented energy/birth assumptions. Do not call that closure a prediction of OH/H/H2/O branching or spectroscopic states.
