The saved particles move across the 10 ps endpoint window. This statement comes from matched particle IDs, rather than the appearance of a density map. Of 29,537 seed IDs, 29,304 occur at both endpoints. Among these survivors, the represented-weight median 3D endpoint displacement is 190.35 um, with p90 378.39 um and p99 490.29 um. The median is about 7.61 transverse grid cells. Only 0.430% of surviving represented weight ends in the same 25 um xy cell.

The movement is mainly transverse over this window: median xy displacement is 190.18 um; median absolute z displacement is 4.22 um (p90 13.08 um). The 1,225 seed survivors in the plotted |z|<0.5 mm slab have 195.92 um median displacement. A separate geometric cohort of 3,945 survivors initially within 100 um of the upper foil has 146.77 um median displacement. These cohorts are defined by initial position, and all displacement statistics are conditional on survival.

For matched survivors, weighted median physical speed changes from 2.04999e+07 to 2.05678e+07 m/s. Weighted mean kinetic energy changes from 1738.70 to 1755.66 eV; median energies are 1198.89 and 1206.87 eV. The weighted mean individual energy change is 16.96 eV, while its median is -1.60 eV. Complete proper-speed, physical-speed, energy and endpoint direction-change distributions are in endpoint-motion.json. Both checkpoints contain only particles with the native electron charge and mass; matched IDs retain identical charge, mass and weight.

IDs distinguish source and loss from survival. The native counter advances by 2,176 new macro IDs. Of those, 1,093 remain at the endpoint and 1,083 were created and lost within the window. Another 233 seed IDs disappear. The exact macro balance is 29,537+2,176-1,316=30,397. Weighted charge ledgers report about 68.186 represented electrons injected, 33.999 absorbed at the lower foil and 7.231 at the upper foil; escaped/short charge increments are zero. These represented physical counts are distinct from macro counts; the endpoints do not assign each lost ID to a particular sink.

Reconstructing the same xy bins used by snapshot(), the normalized height-averaged density shape has total-variation distance 0.1065; the 1 mm midplane slab has 0.4612. Occupied-cell overlap is 0.8530 and 0.4984 respectively. A similar broad density envelope therefore cannot establish that its constituent particles are stationary. These are endpoint population/bin measurements, not a claim of a steady state or a converged physical prediction.

Endpoints do not reveal trajectory, gyro orbit, gyro radius, phase history, or traveled path length. Displacement is a chord; it is only a lower bound on distance traveled by a surviving particle. Lost particles and particles born then lost have no intermediate positions in these checkpoint files. Force-loop E/B values are absent from the checkpoints, so the audit does not attribute this motion to a particular force or infer magnetic confinement from the shape. The separate exact native-field export and a qualified trajectory capture are needed for those questions.

A tracking-only candidate is prepared and compile-checked in tracking-candidate, based on the sealed GPU source without modifying it. Its core changes are five additive lines (include, initial-state hook, velocity copy, post-Boris force hook and end-step hook) plus a new logger header. Removing those exact lines reproduces the original core byte for byte. All existing arithmetic statements, field callback calls, drift/solve ordering, tolerances, source counts and checkpoint header remain unchanged. The logger records at most 32 selected IDs and reads total E/B and pre/post proper velocities already computed in the force loop; it performs no extra field callbacks. It labels absent selected IDs rather than inventing positions. Logged times use the native relative ledger clock; the fixture records the external clock origin.
