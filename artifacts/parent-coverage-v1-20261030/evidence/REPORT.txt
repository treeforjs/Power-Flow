Change
Unsupported electron energies in the parent-ionization channel no longer abort
the exposure (and the joint step). Each (electron, H2O) pair whose target-rest
energy lies where the parent cross section is positive or unknown but no
qualified kernel exists is excluded from the exposure and recorded in an exact
unresolved-coverage ledger: exact pair counts, exact weight sums, distinct
carriers, energy bins, sigma-free exposures and, where a data bound exists, the
upper bound on the reactions not applied (lower bound 0). Nothing is
renormalized, no rate is invented, no unrecorded zero is introduced. The ledger
is part of the RNG/clock state, so it is bound into the native completion,
checkpoint/restore and the full-time seal. A configurable hard limit refuses an
exposure with the typed CoverageRefusal when exceeded.

Coverage map (target-rest energy)
  0-12.621 eV         known zero (v1 parent reference threshold)
  12.621-13.5 eV      unresolved onset gap (Song 2021 parent table starts at
                      13.5 eV); bounded by Munoz 2007 inelastic, 4.665e-21 m^2
  13.5-1000 eV        qualified: Song 2021 Table 12 + parent_ground_equal_pair_CM_v1
  1000-10000 eV       unresolved; bounded by Munoz 2007 inelastic. Existing
                      outer3_BEB_Tan_parent_v1 (v5, carried in v7/v7.1) not
                      admitted: separate binding, weighted-BEB header and a
                      +26.8 % parent seam at 1 keV (4.32e-21 vs 5.476e-21 m^2)
  above 10000 eV      unresolved, no data or bound in any version; refused
                      unless admit_unbounded is set
  v8 H(1s) is an atomic-H target and v7.1 is metadata only; neither supplies
  anything for the onset gap. Equal-pair products of 37.86-39.62 eV incidents
  land in the gap (38.3 eV gives two electrons at 12.838 eV).

Limit (defaults; part of the scope identity)
  refuse iff unbounded pairs present and not admitted, or
          (events_upper > 0.01 per exposure [1.0 cumulative] AND
           unresolved upper-bound share of physical reactions > 1 %)
  0.01 events bounds the probability that the excluded hazard changed the
  sampled exposure; 1 % is below one sixth of the 6 % uncertainty of the applied
  parent table.
