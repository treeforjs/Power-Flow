SS304 phase thermal owner v1.1 -- integration contract (2026-10-31)

Base contract: INTEGRATION-v1.txt (v1 text, unchanged; namespace ss304_phase_v1 there is ss304_phase_v1_1 here, and
the per-cell functions are pq_* instead of pt_*). Everything below is what changes for Hall (the version after v3.2),
the source owner (after v7) and the native owner when they adopt v1.1 instead of v1. Nothing is integrated here.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Hall (after v3.2)

1.1 Construction: as v1, then Config::enable_v1_1(c) (pulse-heating liquid, activities, JANAF internal energy, Knight
    Knudsen layer, radiation, Mn/Si/C composition, melt flow, geometry refusal at 0.5 EM cell). Law domain 298.2-6000 K.
    To reproduce v1 exactly keep the defaults (every v1.1 option off).
1.2 GPU: GpuOptions::session = &hall_session runs the owner on Hall's hall_gpu::Session and stream (no second
    session or context); Hall keeps the session open longer than the owner. The owner compiles its own NVRTC module on
    that session (one extra module).
1.3 New per-step coupling input: StepInput::em_JB[6 e + 0..5] = (J_x, J_y, J_z) in A/m2 and (B_x, B_y, B_z) in T at the
    centre of owner EM cell e (same cell map as em_power_W, INTEGRATION-v1 1.2), from Hall's authoritative midpoint
    fields (cell-averaged current density and |B| at cell centres, as Hall's k_cells already forms). Without it the
    melt flow sees no Lorentz force (recoil, capillary and Marangoni terms still act).
1.4 Ledger columns added to the thermal closure: thermal_delta = Joule + loads + metal_return - evaporation_reference
    - evaporation_kinetic - evaporation_internal - radiation - release_energy (record fields evap_int, radiation).
    Melt flow moves mass and energy between cells inside the owner and adds no column. Not in any ledger: the work of
    the Lorentz force on the melt flow and its viscous dissipation (lubrication model; see README O5). If Hall's
    energy audit needs it, the owner can report sum(f_x q dz) per step (not built).
1.5 Heat digest: Owner::state_sha256() now hashes the block-Merkle roots of the state words (cells: E, six atom words,
    flags; face columns: face mass words) plus ledgers and watermarks; on the GPU engine the leaves are hashed on the
    device (1.4 ms for 262,500 cells) and only 8 words per leaf are downloaded. Hall can seal the thermal state every
    step with it; Owner::verify_state_leaves() recomputes every leaf on the host for audits (downloads the state).
1.6 New refusal kinds Hall must route: melt-flow-step (shorten the step), melt-flow-reynolds (if Config::max_reynolds
    is set), geometry-departure (see 4), phase-explosion. temperature-above-domain is now at 6000 K.
1.7 Cost: 22.6 ms per 10 fs step on the RTX 3080 Ti for the full foil (v1: 6.7 ms). Calling the owner every K field
    steps with StepInput::em_energy_J (accumulated) and the J, B of the last field step keeps the ledgers exact.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Source owner (after v7)
2.1 Back pressure: the Knudsen layer needs the pressure of the gas at each patch surface. The source/native side
    provides StepInput::back_pressure_Pa[p] (Pa) per owner patch, e.g. sum over species of n_s k T_s from the native
    neutral and plasma densities in the first cell layer off the face; a patch at or above the alloy saturation
    pressure stops evaporating (no condensation from the background).
2.2 Surface composition and temperature: PatchReport::surface_atom_fraction (Fe, Cr, Ni, Mn, Si, C) and mean_T; Mn
    dominates evaporation below about 2500 K.
2.3 Water desorption cap (3000 K) and emission-law domain: unchanged from INTEGRATION-v1 2.2/2.3; the foil can now
    reach 6000 K, so the emission law needs an extension or its own typed cap.
2.4 Recoil pressure (PatchReport::recoil_pressure_Pa) is available for a field-emission or plasma-pressure balance.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Native owner (quantized metal vapor, evap_mode = 2)
3.1 Species: six neutral metal species Fe, Cr, Ni, Mn, Si, C (masses 55.845, 51.9961, 58.6934, 54.938043, 28.085,
    12.011 u). Quotes carry six expected counts per patch.
3.2 Internal energy: with gas = 1 every released atom carries internal energy E_int(T_cell) in addition to its kinetic
    energy (electronic excitation and the element/alloy caloric difference); the owner debits it and reports the total
    per patch (PatchReport::released_internal_J). The native owner must carry it on the released macros (one value
    per macro: released_internal_J / n per (patch, element) in the step). A Deposit credits only eps0 + kinetic, so
    the internal energy of a returning atom must be delivered by the native owner as wall heat (a SurfaceLoad) or kept
    in the gas ledger; this is a choice for the native version.
3.3 Recondensation: with knudsen = 1 the Knudsen-layer return is part of the net flux; atoms that the native owner
    tracks back to the foil are deposits, so do not also count a recondensation fraction.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Fixed Hall mesh and what a moving-boundary Hall would need
4.1 What the owner reports now: per face column (slice, face, x column) the outward surface displacement eta (m) =
    mass gained through the face (evaporation, deposits, releases, melt flow) / (rho0 A) + half the column's thermal
    expansion from the density law; PatchReport::displacement_mean_m / displacement_max_abs_m; StepRecord::
    max_displacement_fraction = max |eta| / EM cell thickness. The owner refuses (geometry-departure) when this
    exceeds Config::geometry_refusal_fraction (0.5 in enable_v1_1): beyond half an EM cell the fixed conductor mask
    of Hall no longer represents the foil surface.
4.2 A moving-boundary Hall would need:
    a. a conductor geometry that follows eta: per EM cell a conductor volume fraction (cut cells) or a level set, with
       the conductivity sigma_eff scaled by the filled fraction (the owner can report filled fractions per EM cell);
    b. conservative remapping of E, B and of the conductor/exterior charge banks when cells change between conductor
       and vacuum, with the field energy change booked in the coupled energy ledger;
    c. the native first-hit surfaces moved with the same geometry (ions and electrons collected on the displaced
       surface), and the source owner's patch positions and areas updated;
    d. the owner's thermal grid moving with the surface: the owner already keeps mass-based thicknesses (melt layer)
       and exact transfers, but its cells keep a fixed reference geometry; a moving owner grid needs conservative
       remap of E and atom words between old and new cells (exact in the 128-bit words) and the face-column
       displacement reset after each remap;
    e. melt leaving the foil (droplets, jets) as a new owner of mass and energy in the gap (not modelled);
    f. a time-step control on the displacement per step (the owner's flow_max_fraction plays this role now).
