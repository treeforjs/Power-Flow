3. Reference and carried internal energies are distinct owners
N/source/src/mfem_pic_full_time_chemistry.inc:1077–1087 creates internal_J=0,
reference_J=blob.ref_eV*e. Its registration guard at181–192 currently omits
both values. Generic arena validation permits other finite references and
nonnegative internals, and generic reaction accounting uses them
(mfem_pic_arena.cpp:807–808,1152–1155,1203). Native-v8 chemistry independently
uses blob Q references. Native audit owns an isolated v8.5 guard/reproduction;
no frozen source is altered here. A demonstrated full-v8 energy drift is not
claimed from source inspection alone.

Source neutral Packet::reference_J is zero; its adsorbed-reference removal
is separately charged to the foil. Mapping that source to a negative ATcT
gas reference requires an explicit, count-bound reference convention for the
initial adsorbed inventory and gas, with no invented thermal heat term.
A constant gas chemical reference, per-macro excitation/internal energy,
adsorption/binding cost and electron work-function exchange must not be
conflated or counted twice.

Source v7 permits patch mass_kg and internal_eV; it does not expose a
configurable free-gas chemical reference or a constant chemical reference of
the adsorbed stock. Its packet reference is assigned zero at preparation;
the configured desorption/binding parameters are physical release costs,
not a free reference-gauge knob. A versioned coordinator/source adapter can
in principle retain a constant gas-reference inventory alongside the exact
stock counts: assigning the same per-molecule offset to stock and emitted
gas makes the stock loss and birth credit cancel. It must demonstrate that
cancellation with count-bound operands, persistence and joint publication.
Do not debit the ATcT gas-reference offset as extra electrode heat/cooling,
change a work function to absorb it, or silently add it to excitation energy.

5. SS304 vapour cannot yet be appended without a material contract
T/source/phase_law_host.hpp:25,126 uses molar masses*1e-3/N_A for six elements
Fe,Cr,Ni,Mn,Si,C. Native full_time_metal_vapour_states uses the same listed
atomic weights times 1.66053906660e-27, producing a 3.4558e-10 relative
mass difference. Chemistry Fe/Cr/Ni instead use abundance-weighted isotope
masses (make_production_spec.py:458–472); relative phase-minus-chemistry mass
differences are Fe -2.58738e-6, Cr -6.11780e-7, Ni +9.00072e-7. Exact operands
are retained in mapping.json. No one of these conventions is selected here.

Chemistry has pfc-v3/Fe,Cr,Ni and their singly charged ions, but no Mn/Si/C.
Native metal-v1/Mn,Si,C are nonreacting append-only states; optional
metal-v1/Fe,Cr,Ni would duplicate physical elements under different IDs and
would not automatically become chemistry targets. Never alias their index
positions into the fixed blob registry. Preserve per-element nuclei/inventory
across any extra nonreacting states and classify absent reaction coverage.

The phase owner removes eps0_i + actual K + E_int(T) per evaporated atom
(phase_law.h:18–19; phase_law_host.hpp:168–182). Chemistry neutral metals
have reference zero. FullTimeBirth internal_energy_J can carry released
internal energy, but does not by itself reconcile eps0, chemical reference
and the condensed-material zero. A common material/gas reference ledger and
return/deposition path are required. Phase v1.3 is not coupled into the current
Hall v3.3 coordinator pilot; these are future integration gates.

For metals, a single stock/gas offset is insufficient without relating the
phase law's condensed reference and eps0 to the chemistry neutral reference.
That relation is an explicitly authored material reference convention, not
a kinetic-mass substitution or an additional heating source. Its evaporation
and return offsets must cancel with the actual per-element debits/credits.
